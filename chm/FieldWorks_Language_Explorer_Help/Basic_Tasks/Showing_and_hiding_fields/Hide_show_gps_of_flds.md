# Hide or show groups of fields

*Basic Tasks › Showing and hiding fields*

Some fields are part of a group, such as a lexical [sense](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Sense_field.md), [Affix Template](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/Affix_Templates_field_Category_Edit.md), fieldnote [subrecord](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Subrecords_field.md), and so on.

In these cases, you can click the

- ![](../../assets/images/minus_sign_box.GIF) (minus sign) to collapse that group of fields, or

- ![](../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF) (plus sign) to expand that group of fields.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - [Show Hidden Fields](Show_Hidden_Fields.md) does not affect change whether a group of fields is collapsed or expanded.
>
> - In some cases, such as [Sense](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Sense_field.md) fields, additional content appears next to the field label to help distinguish it when its fields are collapsed.
