# Remove formatting

*Basic Tasks › Formatting Text*

To remove one or more styles from selected text (characters, words, or paragraphs), do the following:

- Select the characters or paragraphs with the style you want to remove.

  - To remove *character* styles, [apply](../../User_Interface/Menus/Format/apply_a_style_to_text.md) the **Default Paragraph Characters** style, or you can also press `Ctrl+Spacebar``.`

  - To remove *paragraph* styles, such as **Numbered List** or **Bulleted List**, apply the **Normal** style.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - **See Also:** [Styles for export](../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Pathway_Configuration_Tool/Styles_for_export.md).
