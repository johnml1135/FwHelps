# About Hunspell

*Basic Tasks › Spell Checking*

- *Hunspell* is a spell checker and morphological analyzer library.

It is the spell checker of LibreOffice, OpenOffice.org, Mozilla Firefox 3 & Thunderbird, and more.

- Two or three [files](dictionary_files.md) are used for each language. You can [get dictionary files](Obtaining_spelling_dictionary_files.md) from the Internet or from other people.

<!-- -->

- Hunspell only supports UTF encodings.
