# Choose Texts

*Basic Tasks › Filtering data*

You can choose which of your texts you want available in **Texts & Words**.

1.  In the Navigation pane, click **Texts & Words**, and then click the tool or view you want to see.

2.  To open the **Choose Texts** dialog box, do any of these steps:

    - On the **View** menu, click ![](../../assets/images/User_Interface/Toolbars/ChooseText.GIF) **Choose Text**.

    - On the **View** toolbar, click ![](../../assets/images/User_Interface/Toolbars/ChooseText.GIF) (**Choose Text**)

    - In the **Title** column (**Interlinear Texts** view, **Texts** pane), click the down arrow (just under the column heading) and then click **Choose**.

    - In the **Form** column that appears in **Word List Concordance** or **Word Analysis**, click the down arrow (just under the column heading) and then click **Choose Texts**.

    - [Export interlinear text](../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Export/Export_Interlinear.md).

3.  In the **Choose Texts** dialog box, do any of these steps:

    - Select (![](../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) or clear (![](../../assets/images/UncheckedBox.PNG)) a node to select or clear *all* the texts under that node.

Nodes have a ![](../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF) or ![](../../assets/images/minus_sign_box.GIF), which allow you to expand or collapse it.

1.  - Select (![](../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) each text you want available.

    - Clear (![](../../assets/images/UncheckedBox.PNG)) each text you want to hide or exclude from the export.

The texts you selected are listed in the **Texts** pane (**Interlinear Texts**), or included in the export document.

Information such as [statistics](../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Statistics_overview.md), [word list column](../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Word_list_columns.md) values and [status bar](../../User_Interface/Toolbars/status_bar.md) totals are based on the texts you choose.

You can [filter](filter_Texts_Words.md) the texts you chose and [sort](../Sorting_data/Sort_Texts_Words.md) them.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - Texts edited in other software applications (programs) appear in the **Choose Texts** dialog box if the *associated lexical project* is the current FieldWorks Language Explorer project. **See Also:** [Sharing tab](../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Project_Properties/Project_Properties_Sharing_tab.md).
>
> - New texts are *not* inserted in the project based on your selections in this dialog box. *However*, if you cleared *all* the check boxes, a new empty text will be created when you click **OK** to close the dialog box.
