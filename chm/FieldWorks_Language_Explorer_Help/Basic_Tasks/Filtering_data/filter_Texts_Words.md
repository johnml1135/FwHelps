# Filter Texts & Words

*Basic Tasks › Filtering data*

## Filter texts

You can filter *texts* in various places, such as the list of texts displayed in the **Texts** pane in **Interlinear Texts** or **Concordance Results** pane in **Concordance**.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then select the desired view (**Interlinear Texts**, **Concordance** and so on).

2.  Do one of the following:

    - To filter data in columns, see [Filter data](filter_data.md).

    - To choose which texts are displayed, see [Choose Texts](Choose_Texts.md).

## Filter words

You can also filter *words* based on which text or texts use them.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then select the desired view (**Word List Concordance**, **Word Analyses** and so on).

    - To filter data in the **Concordance Results** pane, first you need to [specify concordance criteria](../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Concordance/specify_concordance_criteria.md) or [manually specify concordance criteria](../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Concordance/Manually_specify_concordance_criteria.md).

2.  Do the steps in [Filter data](filter_data.md).

> [!NOTE]
>
> - To filter *words* based on which text or texts use them, [configure columns](../Configure_Columns/Configure_Columns_overview.md) to display the [Genres field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/Genres_field_Info.md) in the applicable pane or view (such as **Bulk Edit Wordforms**). Then, [choose](Using_Choose_Items_dialog_box.md) one or more genres to display in that column. Apply additional sorts or filters to other columns as needed.
>
> - For a unique custom filter, consider this: If your language project has more than one analysis writing system and you have *not* used one of those writing systems in an **Info** tab field, such as the **Abbreviation** field or **Source** field, you could enter characters in that unused writing system. Then, [configure a column](../Configure_Columns/Configure_Columns_overview.md) to display content in that writing system and [sort](../Sorting_data/Sort_Texts_Words.md) or filter data based on those characters.
>
> - To filter *complete* analyses from *incomplete* analyses, configure the columns to display **Incomplete Analyses**, and then select **No** to display complete analyses or **Yes** to display incomplete analyses.
>
> - You can [filter texts](filter_Texts_Words.md) in various places, such as the list of texts displayed in the **Texts** pane in **Interlinear Texts** or **Concordance Results** pane in **Concordance**.
>
> - For a given open window, each filter you apply in **Words Analyses** affects **Word List Concordance** and vise versa. **Concordance** and **Bulk Edit Wordforms** are each filtered separately. Filters do not affect other windows that are open to the language project.
