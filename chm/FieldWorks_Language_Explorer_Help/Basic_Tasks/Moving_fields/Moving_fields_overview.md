# Moving fields overview

*Basic Tasks › Moving fields*

[Context-sensitive](../Show_data/Context_sens_menus.md) menus often have a **Move Field** command. You use it to [move a field](Move_a_field.md). This will move the field up or down one position within the set of fields. The **Show Hidden Fields** check box must be selected (![](../../assets/images/CheckedBoxBLACK.png)).

Here are some considerations:

- Your field order data are included each time you use [Send/Receive.](../Collaborating_with_Others/Send_Receive_overview.md)

If you are on a team and share data, the team should agree on the order of the fields.

- You will see **Move Up** and **Move Down**, unless the field is at the top or the bottom of where that field can appear.

- You cannot move a field out if its group.

Examples:

- In [Lexicon Edit](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Lexicon_Edit_fields_overview.md), you cannot move the **Translation** field out of the [Example](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/example_field.md) it belongs to. [Entry-level](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Entry_level_fields_overview.md) fields cannot appear with [Sense-level](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Sense_level_fields_overview.md) fields.

- In [Grammar](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/category_edit_fields_overview.md), you cannot move an Affix Template [Table field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/Table_field.md) from one [named template](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/name_field_template_category_edit.md) to another one. But, you can [move](../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Category_Edit/Move_an_Affix_Template.md) them up or down.

- In [Notebook](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Notebook_fields_overview.md), you cannot move a [Subrecord](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Subrecords_field.md) field into the Record-level [fields](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Notebook_fields_overview.md).

- Some fields can be *different*.

Examples:

- The **Example** field shows **Move Example Up** and **Move Example Down**.

- For an Affix Template, the **Template Name** [field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/name_field_template_category_edit.md) shows **Move Affix Template Up** and **Move Affix Template Down**.

- You cannot move the **Lexical Relations** [field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/lexical_relations_field.md), but you can move fields that hold the various lexical relations.
