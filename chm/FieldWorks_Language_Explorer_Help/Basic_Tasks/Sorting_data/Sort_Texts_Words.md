# Sort Texts & Words

*Basic Tasks › Sorting data*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then select the desired view (**Interlinear Texts**, **Bulk Edit Wordforms** and so on).

2.  Do the steps in [Sort data](Sort_data.md).

> [!TIP]
>
> - If you want to keep certain types of texts together in the **Texts** pane or want to display the texts in a particular order, you can sort and filter by [Genre field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/Genres_field_Info.md) content. Here is another possible option:
>
> - - If your language project has more than one analysis writing system and you have not used one of those writing systems in an **Info** tab field, such as the [Abbreviation field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/Abbreviation_field_Info.md) or [Source field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/Source_field_info.md), you could enter characters in that unused writing system. Then, [configure a column](../Configure_Columns/Configure_Columns_overview.md) to display content in that writing system, and sort or [filter](../Filtering_data/filter_Texts_Words.md) those characters [using Filter for](../Filtering_data/Using_Filter_for.md).
