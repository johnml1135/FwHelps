# Show in from Lexicon

*Basic Tasks › Show data*

In **Lexicon**, [context-sensitive menu](Context_sens_menus.md) commands appear in *many* places and with different wording. They let you "jump" to another area (**Concordance**, **Lists**, another entry and so on) so you can see the data there.

## Samples (*not* exhaustive)

### Lexicon Edit

- **Entries** pane:

  - Right-click a row, and then click to **Show Entry in Concordance**.

- **Entry** pane:

  - [Information bar](../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md) menu button commands include **Show Entry in Concordance**.

  - Menu button ![](../../assets/images/Basic_Tasks/Show_data/menu_button_pic.gif) commands. Examples:

    [Lexeme Form field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Lexeme_Form_field.md) has **Show Lexeme Form in Concordance**

    [Sense field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Sense_field.md) has **Show Sense in Concordance**

    [Variant Form field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Variants_level_fields/Variant_Form_field.md) has **Show Entry in Concordance** (shows variant form)

    [Stem Allomorph](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Alternate_Forms_level_flds/Stem_Allomorph_fld.md) or [Affix Allomorph](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Alternate_Forms_level_flds/Affix_allomorph_fld.md) fields have **Show Allomorph in Concordance**.

  - In the **Lexeme Form** field, you can right-click the lexeme form to see **Show Entry in Concordance** and **Show Lexeme Form in Concordance**. (**See Note** below.)

  - Fields with a reference to another lexical entry, right-click a reference to see available commands. Example: [Variant of field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Variant_of_field.md) has **Show Entry in Lexicon**, **Show Entry in Concordance**, and in some cases, **Show Sense in Concordance**.

  - Some [list reference fields](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Field_Types/List_reference_field.md) allow you to right-click a list item, and then click a command. Examples: [Morph Type field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Morph_Type_Field.md) has **Show in Morphemes Types List** and [Location field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Location_field.md) has **Show in Locations list**. (In some cases, `Ctrl+Click` functions similarly.)

### **Browse** or **Bulk Edit Entries (Lexicon)**

- Right-click a row, and then click **Show Entry in Lexicon**, **Show Entry in Concordance**, or in some cases, **Show Sense in Concordance**.

### **Dictionary**

- Click the entry you want shown in **Lexicon Edit**, and then click **Lexicon Edit** in the **Navigation** **Pane**.

- Click a *referenced headword* to jump to that entry in **Dictionary**.

### **Reversal Indexes**

- Right-click the [Referenced Senses field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Reversal_Indexes_fields/senses_field.md), and click **Show Entry in Lexicon (**`Ctrl+Click` ), **Show Entry in Concordance** or **Show Sense in Concordance**.

- Click a *referenced headword* to jump to that entry in **Reversal Indexes**.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - **Show Entry** concords on the lexeme form and any allomorphs. To concord on a variant, use the menu button next to the [Variant Form](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Variants_level_fields/Variant_Form_field.md) field label, or go to the variant entry in **Lexicon**, then use **Show Entry in Concordance** from there.
