# Show in Lexicon from Interlinear text

*Basic Tasks › Show data*

1.  In a **Gloss** or **Analyze** tab, click the desired word.

    The word appears in a [word focus box](../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Word_Focus_Box_examples.md).

2.  Right-click the **Morphemes**, **Lex Entries**, **Lex Gloss** or **Lex** **Gram Info** line for the particular lexical entry, and then select **Show Entry in Lexicon**.

The window changes to show that lexical entry in **Lexicon Edit**.

> [!TIP]
>
> - Each morpheme of a multimorphemic word usually references a different lexical entry. You can show any of those entries, depending on *where* you right-click your mouse.
>
> - This feature is *particularly useful* if you need to enter multiple lexical forms (such as the phonetic and orthographic lexeme forms) entry because you have words in texts in more than one writing system, such as phonetic and orthographic. **See:** [Baseline text writing systems](../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/baseline_text_writing_systems.md).
