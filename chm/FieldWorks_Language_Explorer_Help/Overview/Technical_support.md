# Technical support

*Overview*

FieldWorks is distributed as freeware. As such, no warranty or support is provided and you use it at your own risk. Because it is under active development, we welcome bug reports, which you can send to <flexerrors@sil.org>. However, when you submit a bug report, we cannot guarantee that you will receive a reply or that the bug will be fixed.

If you are part of a community of FieldWorks users, we encourage you to use your contacts within that community to obtain support, training and advice based on others' experiences.

Other options:

- There is a public discussion group for Language Explorer where you may find useful information about using Language Explorer. You can subscribe to this group at <a href="https://groups.google.com/group/flex-list" style="font-family: Verdana, sans-serif;" target="_blank" title="https://groups.google.com/group/flex-list">https://groups.google.com/group/flex-list</a>. Please tell us how you use, or hope to use, Language Explorer.

- On the task-bar, click the **Start** button, point to **All Programs**, point to **FieldWorks**, and then click **Technical Support**.

- The SIL FieldWorks web site: <a href="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/" target="_blank" title="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/">https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/</a> has

  - downloads (<a href="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/" target="_blank" title="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/">https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/</a>),

  - getting help and training <a href="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/help/" target="_blank" title="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/help/">https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/help/</a>,

  - technical information and support (<a href="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/support/" target="_blank" title="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/support/">https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/support/</a>),

  - **Bantu** Help <a href="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/bantu_features/" target="_blank" title="https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/bantu_features/">https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/bantu_features</a>, and much more.

>
> - For late-breaking information about this version of Language Explorer:\
