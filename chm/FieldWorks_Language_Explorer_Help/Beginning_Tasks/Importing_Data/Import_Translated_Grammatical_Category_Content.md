# Import Translated Grammatical Category Content

*Beginning Tasks › Importing Data*

1.  In the Navigation Pane, click **Grammar**, and then click **Category Edit**.

2.  On the **File** menu, point to **Import**, and then click **Translated Grammatical Category Content**.

    The **Update Grammatical Categories** dialog box appears.

3.  Click **OK**.

    The grammatical category information is imported. This can populate or update your project.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - You do not need to have the **Category Edit** view displayed, but it can be helpful to verify that the results are what you expected and needed.
>
> - Grammatical category information appear in *analysis* writing systems.
>
> <!-- -->
>
> - Some advanced users can open the \*.xml file in [ZEdit](../../Basic_Tasks/ZEdit.md) to examine the grammatical category information. Go to: C:\Program Files\SIL\FieldWorks 9\Templates\GOLDEtic.xml.
