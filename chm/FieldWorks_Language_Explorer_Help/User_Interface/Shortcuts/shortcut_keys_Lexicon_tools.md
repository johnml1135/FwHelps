<td style="width: 52%"><p><code>Tab</code> or <code>Shift+Tab</code> to move the focus into the field, and then type the first letter of the desired list item to select it. Use <code>Tab</code> or <code>Shift+Tab</code> to leave the field.</p>
<p><em>Or</em></p>
<p><code>Tab</code> or <code>Shift+Tab</code> to move the focus into the field, and then press the <code>Spacebar</code> (or <code>Alt+Down Arrow</code>) to open the list.</p>
