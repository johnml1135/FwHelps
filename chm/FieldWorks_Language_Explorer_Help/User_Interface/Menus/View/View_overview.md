# View menu overview

*User Interface › Menus › View*

On the **View** menu, you can do the following to control the way Language Explorer displays the data in your language project:

<table width="100%">
<tbody>
<tr>
<th style="width: 50%"><p>To</p></th>
<th style="width: 50%"><p>Do the following:</p></th>
</tr>
&#10;<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>refresh the current window</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>click <img src="../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Toolbars/RefreshButton.GIF" /> <strong>Refresh</strong> (<code>F5</code>).</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>change the area and view (tool) displayed in the open window (like <a href="../../Toolbars/Navigation/Navigation_Pane_overview.md">Navigation Pane</a>)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>point to an <em>area</em>, and then click a <em>view</em> or <em>tool.</em></p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>turn off <em>all</em> active <a href="../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filtering_data_overview.md">filters</a> (in columns)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>point to <strong>Filters</strong>, and then click <strong>No Filter</strong>.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>turn on a filter which limits the view to a set of imported words (<a href="../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&amp;_Words_tools/Concordance/Concordance_overview.md">Words</a>)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>point to <strong>Filters</strong>, and then click the name of an <a href="../Insert/Import_a_word_set.md">imported word set</a>.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>turn off filters that limit the view to an <a href="../Insert/Import_a_word_set.md">imported word set</a> (<a href="../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&amp;_Words_tools/Concordance/Concordance_overview.md">Words</a>)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>point to <strong>Filters</strong>, and then click <strong>Clear Wordform-Set Filter</strong>.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>show or hide the <a href="../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/About_Lex_Edit_fld_levels.md">Dictionary Preview</a> pane</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>click <strong>Show Dictionary Preview</strong> to show (<img src="../../../assets/images/CheckMark%20in%20RightClick%20Menu.png" />) or hide (<img src="../../../assets/images/Cleared%20RightClick%20Menu.png" />) the pane.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p><a href="../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_hiding_flds_oview.md">show or hide fields</a></p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>click <strong>Show Hidden Fields</strong> to show (<img src="../../../assets/images/CheckMark%20in%20RightClick%20Menu.png" />) or hide (<img src="../../../assets/images/Cleared%20RightClick%20Menu.png" />) hidden fields (similar to selecting or clearing <strong>Show Hidden Fields</strong> on the <a href="../../Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md">Information bar</a>).</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p><a href="../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/Choose_Texts.md">choose texts</a> to show or hide in <strong>Texts &amp; Words</strong> area views</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>click <img src="../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Toolbars/IncludeScr.GIF" /> <strong>Choose Texts</strong>.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" style="width: 50%"><p>If you used <a href="../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&amp;_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Guess_word_breaks.md">Guess Word Breaks</a>, then with a <a href="../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/texts_edit_overview.md">Baseline</a> tab displayed, click <strong>Invisible Spaces</strong> to show (<img src="../../../assets/images/CheckMark%20in%20RightClick%20Menu.png" />) or hide (<img src="../../../assets/images/Cleared%20RightClick%20Menu.png" />) the locations of <em>Zero-width space</em> characters (ZWSP).</p>
<ul>
<li><p>A gray vertical bar shows at each ZWSP location, when selected.</p></li>
<li><p>When you hide the invisible spaces, the <strong>Click Inserts Invisible Space</strong> mode is turned off (<a href="../Insert/Insert_overview.md">Insert</a> menu).</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
