# Undo and Redo

*User Interface › Menus › Edit*

## Reverse the effect of the most recent change

- On the **Edit** menu, click **Undo**.

- On the [Standard toolbar](../../Toolbars/Standard_toolbar.md), click the **Undo** button ![](../../../assets/images/Undo.gif).

- Press the [shortcut keys](../../Shortcuts/Shortcut_keys_to_edit_text.md) `Ctrl+Z`.

## Reverse the effect of the most recent Undo

- On the **Edit** menu, click **Redo**.

- On the [Standard toolbar](../../Toolbars/Standard_toolbar.md), click the **Re****do** button ![](../../../assets/images/Redo.gif).

- Press the [shortcut keys](../../Shortcuts/Shortcut_keys_to_edit_text.md) `Ctrl+Y`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - [Saving](../File/Save.md) clears the Undo and Redo memory.
