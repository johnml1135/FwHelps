# Find a wordform

*User Interface › Menus › Edit*

**Find wordform** searches for wordforms in the [word list](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_list_overview.md).

1.  In **Word List Concordance**, **Word Analyses** or **Bulk Edit Wordforms**, do one of the following:

    - On the toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/Find_wordform.GIF).

    - On the **Edit** menu, click **Find wordform**.

    - Press the [shortcut keys](../../Shortcuts/shortcut_keys_overview.md) `Ctrl+F`.

    The **Find Wordform** dialog box appears.

2.  In the **Writing System** box, select the writing system of the desired wordform.

3.  In the **Find** box, enter the desired wordform.

4.  If the desired wordform does not appear in the **Wordforms** pane, do one of the following:

    - Change the spelling in the **Find** box.

    - Select a different writing system.

    - Click **Cancel** to close the dialog box.

5.  Click the desired wordform, and then click **Go To**.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - A **Target not found** information box appears if a [filter](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filtering_data_overview.md) was turned on that filtered-out the desired word. Click **Yes** to turn off the filter. If you click **No**, you will not see it.
>
> - In **Word List Concordance**, you will see any occurrences in the **Concordance** pane. Then you can click one to see that occurrence in a tab (lower right pane).
>
> - To search for *a* wordform *differently*, such as searching for one of its morphemes or its gloss, you can [manually specify concordance criteria](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Concordance/Manually_specify_concordance_criteria.md) in [Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Concordance/Concordance_overview.md).
