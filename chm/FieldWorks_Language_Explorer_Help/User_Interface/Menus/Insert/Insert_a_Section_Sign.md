# Insert a Section Sign

*User Interface › Menus › Insert*

You might need to insert a Section Sign (§) when you [prepare texts](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Text_preparation_before_analyzing.md).

1.  Click the place in your phrase, sentence or text where you want the insertion point (the place where you want to insert the character).

2.  Press and hold the **Alt** key and type `0167`.

> [!TIP]
>
> You can use the **Character Map** dialog box to learn its keystroke or copy a § (Section Sign) to your computer clipboard.
>
> 1.  On the **Insert** menu, click ![](../../../assets/images/CharMapIcon.png) **Special character**.
>
> The **Character Map** dialog box appears (example below).
>
> 2.  In the **Font** box, select Doulos SIL.
>
> 3.  Select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) **Advanced view**.
>
> 4.  Type the Unicode value **00A7** in the **Go to Unicode** box.
>
> 5.  Click **Select** to put the Section Sign in the **Characters to copy** box.
>
> 6.  Click **Copy** to put the Section Sign on your computer's clipboard.
>
> 7.  Click the place in your phrase, sentence or text where you want the insertion point (the place where you want to insert the character).
>
> 8.  [Paste](../Edit/Cut_copy_and_paste.md) (**Ctrl+V**) it at the location of the insertion point.

### Example

- Notice **U+00A7 Section Sign** and **Keystroke: Alt+0167** appear in the lower corners.
