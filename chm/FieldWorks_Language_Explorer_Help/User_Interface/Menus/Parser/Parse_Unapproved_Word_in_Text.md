# Parse Unapproved Word in Text

*User Interface › Menus › Parser*

The **Parse Unapproved Words in Text** feature uses the parser that is currently selected on the **Parser** menu and parses words in the currently displayed text that don't yet have any approved analyses. This option can help speed up the process when the same words appear repeatedly in the texts.

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words** and then click **Interlinear Texts**.

2.  In the **Texts** pane, click the text that has the words you want to parse.

3.  In the **Text** pane, click the **Analyze** tab.

4.  On the **Parser** menu, click **Parse Unapproved Words in Text**.

    The parser starts (if it was stopped) and then parses any words in the text that don't yet have approved analyses.

> [!TIP]
>
> - The [status bar](../../Toolbars/status_bar.md) displays the status of the parser.
