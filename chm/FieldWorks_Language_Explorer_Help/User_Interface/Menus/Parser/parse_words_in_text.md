# Parse Words in Text

*User Interface › Menus › Parser*

The **Parse Words in Text** feature uses the parser that is currently selected on the **Parser** menu and parses words in the currently displayed text.

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words** and then click **Interlinear Texts**.

2.  In the **Texts** pane, click the text that you want to parse.

3.  In the **Text** pane, click the **Analyze** tab.

4.  On the **Parser** menu, click **Parse Words in Text**.

    The parser starts (if it was stopped) and then parses all the words in the text.

> [!TIP]
>
> - The [status bar](../../Toolbars/status_bar.md) displays the status of the parser.
>
