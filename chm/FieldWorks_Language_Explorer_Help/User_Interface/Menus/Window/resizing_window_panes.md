# Resizing window panes

*User Interface › Menus › Window*

For each open window, you can resize (change the size of) the entire window, or the width of individual panes. In some cases, such as the [Dictionary Preview pane](../View/View_overview.md) in the **Lexicon Edit** tool, or the **Reversals** pane in [Reversal Indexes](../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/reversal_indexes_overview.md), you can change the vertical height of the pane.

- To change the size of an entire open window, do the following:

  - If the window is *maximized* in the display screen, click the **Restore Down** button at the top right corner of the window.

  - Then, click and drag the lower-right corner of the [status bar](../../Toolbars/status_bar.md) until the window is at the desired size.

- To change the width or height of an individual pane, do the following:

  - Hover the mouse pointer over a line between the panes until the pointer becomes two lines with outward pointing arrows, such as ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Window/vertical_lines_w_arrows.GIF) or ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Window/horizontal_lines_w_arrows.GIF). Then, click and hold the mouse button, and drag that edge until the pane has the desired width or height.

  - If the width or height of the pane becomes small enough, the program automatically collapses the window and displays a green arrow, such as ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Window/green_left_arrow.GIF) or![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Window/green_down_arrow.GIF).

    Click the green arrow to restore the pane to the previous width or height.
