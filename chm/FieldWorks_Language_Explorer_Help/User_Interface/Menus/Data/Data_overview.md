# Data overview

*User Interface › Menus › Data*

You use these **Data** menu commands to move to another record (entry, list item, text, and so on):

- Click ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/First.gif) **First Record** to display the first record in the sequence.

- Click ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/Previous.gif) **Previous Record** to display the preceding record.

- Click ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/Next.gif) **Next Record** to display the following record.

- Click ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/Last.gif) **Last Records** to display the last record in the sequence.

In **Texts** **&** **Words**, these commands may also be available:

- **![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/greencheckmark.gif) Approve and Move Next**

  This approves the analysis in the [word focus box](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Word_Focus_Box_examples.md) and moves the focus to the next word or phrase. Equivalent: Click ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/greencheckmark.gif) or press `Enter`.

- **![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/CheckPlusMark.GIF) Approve Throughout this Text**

  This approves the analysis in the word focus box for all instances of the same analysis in the *current* text, and moves the focus to next word or phrase. Equivalent: Click ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/CheckPlusMark.GIF) or press `Ctrl+E`.

- **Approve and Jump to Next Incomplete**

  This approves the analysis in the word focus box and jumps to the next word or phrase with a [suggested](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/interlinear_views_colors.md) or incomplete analysis. Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+J`.

- **Approve and Stay**

  This approves the analysis in the word focus box and keeps the focus on the same word or phrase. Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+S`.

- **Approve** **suggestion** **and**

  Use a submenu command to *approve the analysis* in the current word focus box and move to another word or phrase.

  - **Move Next, Same Line** keeps the insertion point in the *same line* (**Morphemes**, **Word Gloss** and so on) in the word focus box as the focus moves to the next word or phrase. Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+Enter`.

  - **Move Right** - Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+Right Arrow`.

  - **Move Left** - Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+Left Arrow`.

- **Leave suggestion and**

  Use a submenu command to move to another word or phrase *without approving* the [suggestion](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/interlinear_views_colors.md) in the current word focus box. Your manual changes are saved.

  - **Move Next** - Equivalent: Press `Shift+Enter`.

  - **Jump to next** - Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+Shift+J`

  - **Move Next, Same Line** keeps the insertion point in the same line (**Morphemes**, **Word Gloss** and so on) in the word focus box as for focus moves to the next word or phrase. Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+Shift+Enter`.

  - **Move Right** - Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+Shift+Right Arrow`.

  - **Move Left** - Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+Shift+Left Arrow`.

- **![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/link_phrase_button.gif) Make phrase with next word**

  Use this menu command to [link words](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Link_words_in_phrase.md) to form a phrase (available when the current word in the word focus box is followed by another word). Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+M`.

  (If you use the [Text Chart tab](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Text_Chart_tab/Text_chart_overview.md), be careful to only link words that are truly part of a phrase, such as idiomatic phrases. See also: [About entry types](../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/About_Entry_Types.md).)

- **![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/break_phrase_button.gif) Break phrase into** **words**

  Use this menu command to [break a phrase](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Break_a_phrase.md) back into individual words (available when there is a phrase in the current word focus box). Equivalent: Press `Ctrl+W`.

- ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/ApproveAllIcon.png) **Approve All**

Use this menu command (*or* [toolbar button](../../Toolbars/Insert_toolbar.md)) only if you want to approve *all* the analyses i*n the current text* that have a [colored background](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/interlinear_views_background_colors.md). To prevent inadvertent use, this command does *not* have a shortcut key. You can use **Undo**.

> [!TIP]
>
> - You must deliberately approve a *suggested* analysis. Conversely, you must deliberately undo (![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Data/UndoChange.GIF) or `Ctrl+Z`) *manual changes* as all manual changes are saved by default.
>
> - You can [change your opinion of the analysis](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Specify_status_of_analysis.md) later from **Word** **Analyses**.
>
> - With the focus in an empty **Free** or **Lit** line, you can use **Word Glosses** on the [Insert](../Insert/Insert_overview.md) menu or press **Enter** to [copy (insert) word glosses](../Insert/Insert_Word_Glosses.md) into that line.
