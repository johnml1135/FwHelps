# Time of Event field (Notebook)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Notebook*

**Full name:** **Time of Event**

**Location:** In the **Record** pane of **Record Edit** ([Notebook](../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Notebook_overview.md)). It can appear in a record and any subrecords.

**Description:**

This field displays and references an item from the **Time of Day** [list](../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/List_item_usage_table.md).

You can edit the **Time of Day** list and list items. The list usually includes **Predawn**, **Morning**, **Midday**, and so on.

**Tasks:**

- [Choose time of event](../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Record_Edit_overview/Choose_Time_of_Event.md)

- Right-click a time of day reference in the field, and then click **Show in Time of Day list**.

**Field type:** [List Reference](../Field_Types/date_field.md)

**Writing systems:** One or more [analysis](../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)

**Tip:**

- The **Date of Event** field and **Time of Event** field store a date and time (of day) that you can control.

- The date and time for the **Date Created** and **Date Modified** fields are automatically entered by FieldWorks.

- You can change the format of date fields in Microsoft Windows **Control Panel**.
