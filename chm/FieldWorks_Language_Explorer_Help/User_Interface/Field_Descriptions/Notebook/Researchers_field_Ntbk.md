# Researchers field (Notebook)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Notebook*

**Full name:** **Researchers**

**Location:** In the **Record** pane of **Record Edit** ([Notebook](../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Notebook_overview.md)). It can appear in a record and any subrecords.

**Description:**

This field displays and references one or more items from the **People** [list](../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/List_item_usage_table.md). These are individuals who obtained data recorded in this record.

For a record with content in the [Text](Text_field_(Notebook).md) field, the researchers in this field also appear in the [Researchers](../Texts_%26_Words/Researchers_field_(Info).md) field in **Texts & Words**.

**Tasks:**

- [Choose researchers](../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Record_Edit_overview/Choose_researchers.md)

- Right-click a researcher in the field, and then click **Show in People list**.

**Field type:** [List Reference](../Field_Types/List_reference_field.md) (permits type-ahead)

**Writing systems:** One or more [analysis](../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)
