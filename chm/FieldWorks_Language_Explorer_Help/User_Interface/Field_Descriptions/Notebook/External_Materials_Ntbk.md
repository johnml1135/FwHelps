# External Materials (Notebook)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Notebook*

**Full name:** **External Materials**

**Location:** In the **Record** pane of **Record Edit** ([Notebook](../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Notebook_overview.md)). It can appear in a record and any subrecords.

**Description:**

Use this field to reference materials that are related to the record, but that are outside (external) of the FieldWorks project.

- You can insert hyperlinks those materials (pictures, sound files, and so on) if they are accessible to your computer.

- You can also document the existence and location of things that are not stored in or accessible to your computer (artifacts, ruins, recordings, artwork, printed information, and so on).
