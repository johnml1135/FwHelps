# Participants field (Notebook)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Notebook*

**Full name:** **Participants**

**Location:** In the **Record** pane of **Record Edit** ([Notebook](../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Notebook_overview.md)). It can appear in a record and any subrecords.

**Description:**

This field displays and references one or more items from the **People** [list](../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/List_item_usage_table.md) to document who participated in the event.

You can add the roles of the participants (Accused, Accuser, and so on) below this field. Right-click the **Participants** field label, or click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) to see the list of roles you can add. If necessary, [add new roles](../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/Insert_a_list_item.md) to the **Roles** list.

For a record with content in the [Text](Text_field_(Notebook).md) field, the participants in this field also appear in the [Participants](../Texts_%26_Words/Participants_field_(Info).md) field in **Texts & Words**.

**Tasks:**

- [Choose participants and roles](../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Record_Edit_overview/Choose_participants_roles.md)

- Right-click a participant in the field, and then click **Show in People list**.

**Field type:** [List Reference](../Field_Types/List_reference_field.md)

**Writing systems:** One or more [analysis](../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)
