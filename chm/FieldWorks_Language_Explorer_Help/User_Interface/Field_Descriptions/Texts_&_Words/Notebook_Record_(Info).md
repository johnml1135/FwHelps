# Notebook Record (Info)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Texts & Words*

**Full name:** **Notebook Record**

**Location:** **Info** tab ([Interlinear Texts](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/texts_edit_overview.md), [Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Concordance/Concordance_overview.md) and [Word List Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_List_Concordance/Word_List_Concordance_overview.md) tools)

**Description:** This field appears as a header field. Below it are fields used for texts that are stored in a **Notebook** area [Text](../Notebook/Text_field_(Notebook).md) field.

**Tasks:**

Click the ![](../../../assets/images/minus_sign_box.GIF) or ![](../../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF) to collapse or expand the set of fields under **Notebook Record**.

To see the record in **Notebook** that has the current text in a **Text** field, click this field, and then do one of the following:

- Click the **Show Record in Notebook** link.

- Click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)), and then click **Show Record in Notebook**.

**Field type:** Not editable.

**Writing systems:** Not applicable.
