# Genres field (Info)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Texts & Words*

**Full name:** **Genres**

**Location:** **Info** tab ([Interlinear Texts](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/texts_edit_overview.md), [Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Concordance/Concordance_overview.md) and [Word List Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_List_Concordance/Word_List_Concordance_overview.md) tools)

**Description:** This field displays and references the [name](../Lists/Genres_fields/Name_field_Genres.md) of one or more items from the **Genres** [list](../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/List_item_usage_table.md) which categorizes the text (and its words) by genre.

**Tasks:**

- [Configure columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Using_Configure_Columns_dialog_box.md) to display the genres in various places (**Texts** pane in **Interlinear Texts**, the **Concordance Results** pane, **Bulk Edit Wordforms** and so on). Then, [sort](../../../Basic_Tasks/Sorting_data/Sorting_data_overview.md) or [filter texts or words](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filter_Texts_Words.md) *by genre*.

- [Enter text metadata](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Enter_text_metadata.md)

- **See also:** [Texts & Words overview](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Texts_and_Words_overview.md)

**Field type:** [List reference](../Field_Types/List_reference_field.md) field

**Writing Systems:** One or more [analysis](../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)

**Note:**

What is in the [Genre list](../Lists/Genres_fields/Genres_fields_overview.md) are standard categories provided by linguists in the field of Discourse Analysis. It is, however, quite common to find skewing and embedding of these categories within texts. Stories are told for Normative reasons (that is, to enforce Norms of society), stories often contain conversations, and so on.

**Example:**

Suppose you have an interview with a male government official about a program detailing the success of a water conservation program. Consider the following:

- Was he telling the story of how the water project came into being?

  (could be narrative)

- Was he describing what the water conservation project is?

  (could be expository)

- Was he describing how the water conservation project is a good thing for the community?

  (could be hortatory; normative, behavioral)
