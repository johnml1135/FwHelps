# Word Gloss field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Texts & Words*

**Full name:** **Word Gloss**

**Location:** **Word Gloss** fields appear in the **Wordform Analyses** pane ([Word Analyses](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md)) for analyses that are under **User Approved (Analyses)**. (This field is hidden for analyses below **User Opinion Unknown** or **User Disapproved**.)

**Description:**

You *can* [add a word gloss](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Add_Word_Gloss.md) in **Word Analyses**.

However typically, each field displays a word gloss added while the current wordform was [glossed](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Analyze_Text_overview.md). Glosses can be added in multiple analysis writing systems in this field, or in the [word focus box](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Word_Focus_Box_examples.md) ([configured](../../Menus/Tools/Configure_interlinear_lines_dialog_box.md) *separately*).

Edits you make in this field appear in [Interlinear Texts](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/texts_edit_overview.md) for each occurrence of the word with the current analysis.

**Field type:** [Single-line text field](../Field_Types/Single_line_text_field.md) – you cannot embed characters in other writing systems or embed styles.

**Writing systems:** One or more [analysis](../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)

**Tip:**

A word gloss or word category used for a wordform is *not* added to the lexicon. It is stored as part of the [word list](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Word_list_overview.md) and remains available to be proposed for glossing of the next occurrence of that same wordform.

- If you will only develop a lexicon, there may be less use for the word-level glosses.

- If you will develop a text corpus to show or to learn how words are used in context, the word-level glosses probably will be useful–maybe more useful than the morpheme level.
