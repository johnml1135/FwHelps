# Abbreviation field (Info)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Texts & Words*

**Full name:** **Abbreviation**

**Abbreviation:** **Abbr**

**Location:** **Info** tab ([Interlinear Texts](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/texts_edit_overview.md), [Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Concordance/Concordance_overview.md) and [Word List Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_List_Concordance/Word_List_Concordance_overview.md))

**Description:** This field stores an abbreviation of the [Title](Title_field_Info.md) for the current text. It appears in the **Abbreviation** column, the **Ref** columns, and also in the [Reference field](../Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Reference_field.md) if you used [Find example sentence](../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Find_example_sentence.md).

**Tasks:**

- [Configure columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Using_Configure_Columns_dialog_box.md) to display the abbreviation:

  - The **Texts** pane (in **Interlinear Texts**) has an **Abbreviation** column.

  - The **Concordance Results** pane (in **Concordance**) and the upper-right pane in **Word List Concordance** both display the abbreviation in a **Ref** [column](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_list_columns.md) (it appears with a numerical reference to the paragraph and line).

- [Enter text metadata](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Enter_text_metadata.md).

- [Sort](../../../Basic_Tasks/Sorting_data/Sort_Texts_Words.md) and [filter texts and words](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filter_Texts_Words.md) by content in this field.

- **See also:** [Texts & Words overview](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Texts_and_Words_overview.md)

**Field type:** Editable [single-line text](../Field_Types/Single_line_text_field.md). It does *not* allow you to embed other writing systems or styles.

**Writing Systems:** All [vernacular and analysis](../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)
