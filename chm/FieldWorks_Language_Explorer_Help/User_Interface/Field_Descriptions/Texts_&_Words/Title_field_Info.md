# Title field (Info)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Texts & Words*

**Full name:** **Title**

**Location:** **Info** tab ([Interlinear Texts](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/texts_edit_overview.md), [Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Concordance/Concordance_overview.md) and [Word List Concordance](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_List_Concordance/Word_List_Concordance_overview.md) tools)

**Description:**

This field stores the title (name) of the current text.

The **Title** box that appears below the **Text** pane [Information bar](../../Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md) in **Interlinear Texts** is a different view of this field. However, more writing systems may be available in this field than in the **Title** box.

Contents in the field appear in **Title** columns. If there is *no* content in the [Abbreviation](Abbreviation_field_Info.md) field, then content from the **Title** field appears in the **Ref** [column](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_list_columns.md) or **Reference** fields in panes where [occurrences](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Concordance/Manually_specify_concordance_criteria.md) are listed. The [Find example sentence](../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Find_example_sentence.md) feature works this way.

**Tasks:**

- [Enter text metadata](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Enter_text_metadata.md)

- [Configure columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Using_Configure_Columns_dialog_box.md) in the tools listed above to display the title.

- [Sort](../../../Basic_Tasks/Sorting_data/Sort_Texts_Words.md) and [filter texts and words](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filter_Texts_Words.md) to show texts by title.

- **See also:** [Texts & Words overview](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Texts_and_Words_overview.md)

**Field type:** Editable [single-line text](../Field_Types/Single_line_text_field.md). It does *not* allow you to embed other writing systems or styles.

**Writing systems:** All [vernacular and analysis](../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)
