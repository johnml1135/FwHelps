# Wordform field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Texts & Words*

**Full name:** The field name (label) is the current *wordform*.

**Location:** At the top of the **Wordform Analyses** pane ([Word Analyses](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md))

**Description:**

This field references content from the **Form** column. It displays it as the field label.

Note: You can [configure columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Using_Configure_Columns_dialog_box.md) to show other vernacular writing systems in separate **Form** columns without affecting this field.

**Tasks:**

Right-click near the wordform, and then use the link or a menu command:

- **Assign Analysis** opens the [Analysis Usage](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Assign_analysis_usage.md) dialog box.

- **Change Spelling** [opens](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Change_Spelling/Open_Change_Spelling_dialog_box.md) the **Change Spelling** dialog box.

- **Delete Wordform** [deletes](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Delete_a_wordform.md) the wordform from the [word list](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_list_overview.md).

**See also:** [Field Visibility](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/change_the_visibility_of_fields.md) and [Texts & Words overview](../../../Using_Tools/Texts_%26_Words_tools/Texts_and_Words_overview.md).

**Field type:** A unique variation of a list-reference field

**Writing systems:** *Default* (top) vernacular only
