# Nested Group Members field (Ad hoc Rules)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Grammar › Ad hoc Rules fields*

**Full name:** **Nested Group** **Members**

**Location:** **Ad hoc Rules** ([Grammar](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/grammar_overview.md)) for [ad hoc groups](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/About_Ad_hoc_Groups.md), right pane

**Description:**

This field acts as a heading for nested groups of ad hoc rules.

Groups help you organize the set of ad hoc rules - you can collect a number of them and give them an appropriate label (such as "Prevent unwanted parses"). Under such a grouping, one could have nested items like "Stem versus component parts" and "Unexpected allomorph sequences."

**Tasks:**

- Click the ![](../../../../assets/images/minus_sign_box.GIF) (minus sign) to hide the fields associated with nested group members; click the ![](../../../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF) (plus sign) to show them.

- Click or right-click the **Nested Group** **Members** field label to display links and a menu button ![](../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) with these menu commands:

  - [Insert Morpheme Rule](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/Insert_a_morpheme_ad_hoc_rule.md)

  - [Insert Allomorph Rule](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/insert_an_allomorph_ad_hoc_rule.md)

  - [Insert Nested Group](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/insert_an_ad_hoc_group.md)

  - **See:** [Ad hoc Co-occurrence Prevention Rules overview](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/Ad_hoc_Rules_overview.md)

**Field type:** Not Editable

**Writing systems:** Not Applicable

**Grammar Sketch:** Under **Residue**

>
> - For a discussion about ad hoc rules, point to **Resources** on the [Help](../../../Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu, and then click **Introduction to Parsing**.
