# Members field (Ad hoc Rules)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Grammar › Ad hoc Rules fields*

**Full name:** **Members**

**Location:** **Ad hoc Rules** ([Grammar](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/grammar_overview.md)) for [ad hoc groups](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/About_Ad_hoc_Groups.md), right pane

**Description:**

The **Members** field acts as a heading for one or more groups of ad hoc rules. Under this field, sets of [Key Morpheme](Key_Morpheme_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) or [Key Allomorph](Key_Allomorph_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md), [Cannot Occur](Cannot_Occur_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md), and [Other Morpheme(s)](other_morphemes_field_ad_hoc_rules.md) or [Other Allomorph(s)](other_allomorphs_field_ad_hoc_rules.md) appear.

For nested groups, the [Nested Group Name](Nested_Group_Name_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md), [Nested Group Description](Description_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) and [Nested Group Members](nested_group_members_field_ad_hoc_rules.md) fields also appear under this field.

**Tasks:**

- Click the ![](../../../../assets/images/minus_sign_box.GIF) (minus sign) to hide the fields associated with the member; click the ![](../../../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF) (plus sign) to show them.

- Click or right-click the **Members** field label to display links and a menu button ![](../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) with these menu commands:

  - [Insert Morpheme Rule](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/Insert_a_morpheme_ad_hoc_rule.md)

  - [Insert Allomorph Rule](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/insert_an_allomorph_ad_hoc_rule.md)

  - [Insert Nested Group](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/insert_an_ad_hoc_group.md)

  - **See:** [Ad hoc Co-occurrence Prevention Rules overview](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/Ad_hoc_Rules_overview.md)

**Field type:** Not Editable

**Writing systems:** Not Applicable

**Grammar Sketch:** Not Applicable

>
> - For a discussion about ad hoc rules, point to **Resources** on the [Help](../../../Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu, and then click **Introduction to Parsing**.
