# Right Headed field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Grammar › Compound Rules fields*

**Full name:** **Right Headed**

**Location:** **Compound Rules** ([Grammar](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/grammar_overview.md)) for *headed* compounds, right pane

**Description:**

- When selected (![](../../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)), the [Right Member](right_member_field.md) is the head of the compound.

- When cleared (![](../../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Tools/UncheckedBox.PNG)), the [Left Member](left_member_field.md) is the head of the compound.

The choice you make here affects the [parsers](../../../Menus/Parser/Parsing_words_overview.md).
