# Override Head with field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Grammar › Compound Rules fields*

**Full name:** **Override Head with**

**Location:** **Compound Rules** ([Grammar](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/grammar_overview.md)) for *left- or right-headed* compound rules, right pane

**Description:** This field appears and acts as a heading to identify the use of the category in the [Category field](Category_field_Compound_Rules.md) immediately below it.

**Tasks:**

- [Insert a compound rule](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Compound_Rules/Insert_a_compound_rule.md)

- [Delete a compound rule](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Compound_Rules/Delete_a_Compound_Rule.md)

- [Edit a compound rule](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Compound_Rules/Edit_a_Compound_Rule.md)

- **See** **also****:** [Compound Rules overview](../../../../Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Compound_Rules/Compound_Rules_overview.md)

**Field type:** Not Editable

**Writing systems:** Not Applicable

**Grammar Sketch:** Not Applicable

**Tip:** For more information, on the [Help](../../../Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu, point to **Resources** and then click **Introduction to Parsing**. See the section **Incorporation as a Headed Compound with Override**.
