# People fields overview

| [Full Name](full_name_field_people.md)                               |

| [Positions](Positions_field_People.md)                               |

> [!NOTE]
>
> - The **People** list fields allow you to be very specific and thorough as you describe the individuals in your list. This may be particularly important to document individuals listed in your [fieldnote records](../../../../Using_Tools/Notebook_tools/Notebook_overview.md). **Positions** is just one of the fields.
>
> <!-- -->
>
> - In the current version, you are allowed to select **Support hierarchy** in the **Configure Lists** dialog box. However, you cannot insert subitems in this list.
