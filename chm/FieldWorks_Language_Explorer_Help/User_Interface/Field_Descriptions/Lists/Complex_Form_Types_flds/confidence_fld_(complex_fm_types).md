# Confidence field (Complex Form Types)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lists › Complex Form Types*

**Full name:** **Confidence**

**Location:** **Complex Form Type** pane of the **Complex Form Types** list ([Lists](../../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/List_item_usage_table.md))

**Description:** This field references and displays the [name](../Confidence_Levels_fields/Name_field_Confidence_Levels.md) of an item from the **Confidence Levels** list. This indicates your confidence in the current [named](name_fld_(complex_fm_types).md) complex form type.
