# Referenced Complex Forms (Publication Settings)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Publication Settings level fields*

<table width="100%">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="width: 20%"><p><strong>Full name:</strong></p></td>
<td style="width: 80%"><p><strong>Referenced Complex Forms</strong></p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 20%"><p><strong>Location:</strong></p></td>
<td style="width: 80%"><p>In the <strong>Entry</strong> pane (<strong>Lexicon Edit</strong>).</p>
<p>This field is at the <a href="Publ_Set_level_fields_overview.md">Publication Settings level</a>.</p>
<p>(A separate <strong>Referenced Complex Forms</strong> field appears in each <a href="../Sense_level_fields/Sense_level_fields_overview.md">sense</a> and subsense.)</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 20%"><p><strong>Description:</strong></p></td>
<td style="width: 80%"><p>This field references and displays entries which are complex forms</p>
<ul>
<li><p>that use this entry (<em>not</em> a particular sense or subsense) as a <a href="../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Choose_components_for_Components_field.md">component</a>,</p></li>
<li><p><em>and</em>, that you want to make available for display in <strong>Dictionary</strong>.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>There is a corresponding <strong>Complex Forms</strong> field that displays and references all complex forms that use this entry (not a specific sense or subsense) as a component. However, the <strong>Complex Forms</strong> field does <em>not</em> affect what is displayed in <strong>Dictionary</strong>. Complex forms displayed in the <strong>Referenced Complex Forms</strong> field are available for display in <a href="../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Dictionary/Dictionary_overview.md">Dictionary</a> as <a href="../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Specify_publishable_lexical_data.md">publishable lexical data</a>.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 20%"><p><strong>Tasks:</strong></p></td>
<td style="width: 80%"><ul>
<li><p><a href="../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Choose_referenced_complex_forms.md">Choose referenced complex forms</a></p></li>
<li><ul>
<li><p>Select an entry in this field, and then press the <strong>Delete</strong> or <strong>Backspace</strong> key to remove it from the field.</p></li>
</ul></li>
<li><p>Right-click an entry in the field, and then use <strong>Move Left</strong> or <strong>Move Right</strong> to manually reorder the entries. <em>This affects the</em> <strong>Dictionary</strong> <em>view order.</em></p></li>
<li><p>Click the menu button (<img src="../../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF" />), and then click <strong>Alphabetical Order</strong> to set the order of the entries to be alphabetical. <em>This affects the</em> <strong>Dictionary</strong> <em>view order</em>.</p></li>
<li><p>You can <a href="../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md">configure the dictionary</a> to show content from associated fields when you configure <a href="../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Referenced_Complex_Forms.md">Referenced Complex Forms</a>. When you do this, the order of the <strong>Complex Form Types</strong> list determines the sort order by type. Then, the order of complex forms in the field is applied.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 20%"><p><strong>Field type:</strong></p></td>
<td style="width: 80%"><p>Non-editable</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 20%"><p><strong>Writing systems:</strong></p></td>
<td style="width: 80%"><p>Default <a href="../../../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md">vernacular</a></p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" style="width: 20%"><p><strong><img src="../../../../../assets/images/Important_Icon.gif" /> Important</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><p><strong><a href="../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Other_Referenced_Complex_Forms.md">Other Referenced Complex Forms</a> are forms that are in the <a href="Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md">Referenced Complex Forms</a> field but</strong> <em>not also</em> <strong>in the <a href="Subentries_(Publication_Settings).md">Subentries</a> field.</strong></p></li>
<li><p>Entries that are complex forms (have components) can also be components of other complex forms. So, even entries that are complex forms have <strong>Referenced Complex Forms</strong> fields.</p></li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
