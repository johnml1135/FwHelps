# Dialect Labels (Entry) (Variants Level)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Variant level fields*

**Full name:** **Dialect Labels (Entry)**

**Location:** **Entry** pane (**Lexicon Edit**), at the [Variants level](Variant_level_fields_overview.md) (for entries that *have variants*)

**Description:**

In an entry that *has a variant*, this field references and displays the [abbreviation](../../../Lists/Dialect_Labels/Abbreviation_field_(Dialect_Labels).md) of one or more items from the **Dialect Labels** [list](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/List_item_usage_table.md). It identifies the dialect or dialects of the variant form.

In the *variant* *entry*, the dialect labels you choose in *this* field also appear in the **Dialect Labels (Entry)** [field](../Entry_level_fields/Dialect_Labels_Entry.md) which is at the [entry level](../Entry_level_fields/Entry_level_fields_overview.md).

**Tasks:**

- [Choose Dialect Labels](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Choose_a_dialect_label.md)

If you chose more than one dialect label, you can right-click one of them and then click **Move Right** or **Move Left**. The order in the field affects the order in **Dictionary**.

- Right-click a dialect label and then click **Show** to see it in **Lists**.

- You can [configure the dictionary](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to show the [abbreviation](../../../Lists/Dialect_Labels/Abbreviation_field_(Dialect_Labels).md) or full name when you configure [Dialect Labels](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dialect_Labels.md).

**Field type:** [List reference field](../../../Field_Types/List_reference_field.md)

**Writing systems:** Best [vernacular or analysis](../../../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)
