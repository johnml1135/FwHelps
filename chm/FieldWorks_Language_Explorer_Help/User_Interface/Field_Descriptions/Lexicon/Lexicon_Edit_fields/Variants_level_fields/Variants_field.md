# Variants field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Variant level fields*

**Full name:** **Variants**

**Location:** In the **Entry** pane (**Lexicon Edit**), it is the top [Variants level field](Variant_level_fields_overview.md).

**Description:** This field acts as a header below which one or more variant forms of the headword and their associated fields appear.

**Tasks:**

- Click the ![](../../../../../assets/images/minus_sign_box.GIF) (minus sign) to collapse the group of fields; click the ![](../../../../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF) (plus sign) to expand them.

- Click or right-click the field label to display a menu button ![](../../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that opens a menu with commands you can use. The **Insert Variant** command also appears as a hyperlink.

- [Insert a variant form](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Insert_a_variant_form.md).

- [Choose Variant of entry](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Choose_Variant_of_entry.md)

- [Delete a variant form](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Delete_a_variant_form.md)

- You can [configure the dictionary](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to include variant information when you configure [Variant Forms](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Variant_Forms.md).

**Field type:** Not Editable (header)

**Writing systems:** Not Applicable

###  Tip — Allomorph or variant?

- An allomorph is one of two or more complementary morphs which manifest a morpheme in its different phonological or morphological environments.

  - When deciding between calling something an allomorph or a variant:\
    [Allomorphs](../Alternate_Forms_level_flds/Alternate_Forms_fld.md) if it is phonologically conditioned, otherwise make it a variant for morphologically conditioned or lexically conditioned suppletion.

1.  - For a discussion about allomorphs and variants, point to **Resources** on the [Help](../../../../Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu and then click **Introduction to Lexicography.**
