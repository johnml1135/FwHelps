# Subentries field (Sense)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Sense level fields*

**Full name:** **Subentries**

**Location:**

**Entry** pane of **Lexicon Edit**, in *each* [sense](Sense_level_fields_overview.md) and subsense

(A *separate* **Subentries** field appears at the [Publication Settings level](../Publication_Settings_flds/Publ_Set_level_fields_overview.md).)

**Description:**

In a sense or subsense, it references and displays lexical entries which are complex forms

- that use this sense or subsense as a [component](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Choose_components_for_Components_field.md),

- *and*, that you want to make available for display in **Dictionary**.

Root-based and hybrid [layouts](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md) are affected by this field.

**Tasks:**

- [Choose subentries for Subentries field](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Choose_subentry_for_Subentries_field.md).

<!-- -->

- The order of the entries *here* also affects order in **Dictionary**. Set the order in either of these ways:

  - Right-click an entry in the field, and then click **Move Left** or **Move Right** to manually reorder the entries.

  - Right-click the field label, and then click **Alphabetical Order** to set the order of the entries to be alphabetical.

<!-- -->

- You can [configure the dictionary](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to include the [subentries](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Subentries.md) or [minor subentries](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Minor_Subentries_(Hybrid_view).md). When you do this, the order of the **Complex Form Types** list determines the sort order by type. Then, the order of complex forms in the field is applied.

- Right-click an entry in the field, and then click any of the following:

  - **Show Entry in Lexicon**

  - **Show Entry in Concordance**

**Tip:**

- For *lexeme*-based [layouts](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md) **see:** [Referenced Complex Forms field](../Publication_Settings_flds/Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md).

- **For** *root***-based and** *hybrid* **layouts, [Other Referenced Complex Forms](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Other_Referenced_Complex_Forms.md) are those forms that appear in the [Referenced Complex Forms](../Publication_Settings_flds/Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md) field, but which are** *not* **also in the [Subentries](../Publication_Settings_flds/Subentries_(Publication_Settings).md) field.**

<!-- -->

- **E**ntries that are complex forms can also be components of other complex forms. So, even entries that are complex forms have **Subentries** fields.
