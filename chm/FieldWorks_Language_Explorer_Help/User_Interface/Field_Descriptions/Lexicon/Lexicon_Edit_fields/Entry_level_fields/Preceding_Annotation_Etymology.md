# Preceding Annotation (Etymology)

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Entry level fields*

**Full name:** **Preceding Annotation**

**Location:**

In the **Entry** pane (**Lexicon Edit**).

This field is between the **Lexeme Form** [field](Lexeme_Form_field.md) and the **Sense 1** [field,](../Sense_level_fields/Sense_field.md) at the [entry level](Entry_level_fields_overview.md).

It is part of the set of **Etymology** [fields](Etymology_fields_overview.md).

**Description:**

This field stores content that will introduce the etymology or borrowing. It will appear *before* the other etymological information.

You could use a symbol, such as \<. Or, you could use a word or phrase, such as *from*, *borrowed from* or *possibly*.

**Tasks:**

- [Enter etymological information](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Enter_etymological_information.md)

- Right-click the field label, or click the menu button (![](../../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)), and then do either of the following:

  - point to **Writing Systems** and then click [Configure](../../../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_Writing_Systems/Show_WSs_overview.md)

  - point to **Field Visibility** and then click the desired [visibility](../../../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/change_the_visibility_of_fields.md) option.

- You can [configure the dictionary](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to include content from this field when you configure [Etymology](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Etymology.md).

**Field type:** [Single-line text field](../../../Field_Types/Single_line_text_field.md) – you can embed characters in other writing systems or embed styles

**Writing systems:** One or more [analysis](../../../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md) writing system
