# Complex Forms field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Entry level fields*

**Full name:** **Complex Forms**

**Location:**

In the **Entry** pane (**Lexicon Edit**).

This field is between the **Lexeme Form** [field](Lexeme_Form_field.md) and the **Sense 1** [field](../Sense_level_fields/Sense_field.md), at the [entry-level](Entry_level_fields_overview.md).

(There is another **Complex Forms** field in each [sense](../Sense_level_fields/Sense_level_fields_overview.md) and subsense.)

**Description:** This field references and displays the complex form entries that use this entry as a [component](Components_field.md).

**Tasks:**

- Click the field and then click the ellipsis button (![](../../../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG)) to open the **Find Lexical Entry** dialog box. From there, [find](../../../../Menus/Edit/Find_a_lexical_entry.md) or [create](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Create_a_lexical_entry.md) a complex form to add to this field.

- Select a complex form in this field, and then press the **Delete** or **Backspace** key to remove it from the field.

- Right-click a complex form in this field, and then click any of the following:

  - **Show Entry in Lexicon**

  - **Show Entry in Concordance**

  - **Referenced Complex Forms** - click to select (![](../../../../../assets/images/CheckMark%20in%20RightClick%20Menu.png)) and add the entry into the [Referenced Complex Forms](../Publication_Settings_flds/Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md) field; click to clear (![](../../../../../assets/images/Cleared%20RightClick%20Menu.png)) and remove the entry from the **Referenced Complex Forms** field.

  - To reorder the complex forms in the *field*, click the menu button (![](../../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) or right-click the field label, and then click **Alphabetical Order**.

To change their order in **Dictionary**, see [Referenced Complex Forms](../Publication_Settings_flds/Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md) and [Subentries](../Publication_Settings_flds/Subentries_(Publication_Settings).md).

- You can [configure the dictionary](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to show associated fields when you configure [Complex Forms](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Complex_Forms.md).

**Field type:** Non-editable

**Writing systems:** Default [vernacular](../../../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)
