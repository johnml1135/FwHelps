# Stem Allomorph field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Allomorphs level fields*

**Full name:** **Stem Allomorph**

**Abbreviation:** **al**

**Location:**

In the **Entry** pane (**Lexicon Edit**).

This field is at the [Allomorphs level](Alt_Forms_lev_flds_ov.md) for each allomorph.

**Description:**

This field stores a stem allomorph of the headword ([Lexeme Form](../Entry_level_fields/Lexeme_Form_field.md) or [Citation Form](../Entry_level_fields/Citation_Form_field.md)).

[Parsers](../../../../Menus/Parser/Parsing_words_overview.md) use allomorphs.

**Tasks:**

- [Delete an allomorph](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/delete_an_alternate_form.md)

- [Insert an allomorph](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Insert_an_alternate_form.md)

- [Merge allomorphs](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Merge_allomorphs.md)

- [Move an allomorph](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Move_Allomorph.md) (to reorder them)

- [Show Allomorph in Concordance](../../../../../Basic_Tasks/Show_data/Show_in_from_Lexicon.md)

- [Swap Lexeme Form with Allomorph](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Swap_LexForm_with_Allomorph.md)

- You can [configure the dictionary](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Using_the_Configure_Dictionary_dialog_box.md) to display [allomorphs](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/alternate_forms.md) and related information.

**Field type:** [Single-line text field](../../../Field_Types/Single_line_text_field.md) - you *cannot* embed characters in other writing systems or embed styles

**Writing systems:** One or more [vernacular](../../../../../Advanced_Tasks/Writing_Systems/Add_a_new_writing_system/About_Writing_Systems.md)

**Note:**

Allomorph order in *very important* for [parsers](../../../../Menus/Parser/Parsing_words_overview.md).

When you insert a *stem* allomorph, the **Stem Allomorph** field, and associated [Is Abstract Form](Is_Abstract_Form_fld_Allomorph.md), [Morph Type](Morph_type_fld_allomorph.md), [Environments](Environments_fld_allomorph.md) and [Stem Allomorph Labels](Stem_Allomorph_Label_fld_Allomorph.md) fields appear.

For more information, on the [Help](../../../../Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu point to **Resources**, and then click **Introduction to Parsing**. Stem Allomorph Labels are discussed in section 3. Section 4 includes a discussion about [morpheme types](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lists_tools/About_Morpheme_Types.md) that are significant to the parser.
