# Alllomorphs field

*User Interface › Field Descriptions › Lexicon › Lexicon Edit fields › Allomorphs level fields*

**Full name:** **Allomorphs**

**Location:**

In the **Entry** pane (**Lexicon Edit**).

This field is the top field at the [Allomorphs-level](Alt_Forms_lev_flds_ov.md).

**Description:** This field acts as a header below which one or more **Stem Allomorph** [field](Stem_Allomorph_fld.md) or **Affix Allomorph** [fields](Affix_allomorph_fld.md) and their associated fields appear. These fields are used with allomorphs of morphemes.

**Tasks:**

- Click the ![](../../../../../assets/images/minus_sign_box.GIF) (minus sign) to collapse the group of fields; click the ![](../../../../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF) (plus sign) to expand them.

- Click or right-click the field label to display a menu button ![](../../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that opens a menu with commands you can use. The **Insert Allomorph** command also appears as a hyperlink.

- [Insert an allomorph](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Insert_an_alternate_form.md)

- [Insert an Affix Process](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Insert_Affix_Process_Rule.md)

- [Convert an existing Allomorph into an Affix Process](../../../../../Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Convert_existing_form_or_allomorph.md)

- You can [configure the dictionary](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to include the allomorph and associated data when you configure [Allomorphs Forms](../../../../Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/alternate_forms.md).

- You can [add custom fields](../../../../Menus/Tools/Custom_Fields/add_a_custom_field.md) to allomorphs.

**Field type:** Not Editable (header)

**Writing systems:** Not Applicable

###  Tip — Allomorph or variant?

- An allomorph is one of two or more complementary morphs which manifest a morpheme in its different phonological or morphological environments.

  - When deciding between calling something an allomorph or a variant:\
    *Allomorphs* if it is phonologically conditioned, otherwise make it a <a href="../Variants_level_fields/Variant_level_fields_overview.md" style="font-family: Verdana, sans-serif;">variant</a> for morphologically conditioned or lexically conditioned suppletion.

  - For a discussion about allomorphs and variants, point to **Resources** on the [Help](../../../../Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu and then click **Introduction to Lexicography.**
