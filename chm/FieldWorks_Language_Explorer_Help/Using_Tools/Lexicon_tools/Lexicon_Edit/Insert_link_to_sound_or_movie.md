# Insert a sound or movie file

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

For each [Pronunciation](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Pronunciation_field.md) field, you can insert links to one or more sound or movie files. You can [play](play_sound_or_movie.md) the file.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, if you cannot see the desired **Pronunciation** field, [show hidden fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md).

4.  Click the **Pronunciation** field, and then do one of the following:

    - Click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)), and then click **Insert Sound or Movie**.

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Insert/Insert_overview.md) menu, click **Sound or Movie**.

    - Click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG) on the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md), and then click **Insert Sound or Movie File**.

    The **Choose Sound or Movie File** dialog box appears.

5.  Select the desired file, and then click **Open**.

    If the file you selected is *not* in an appropriate [folder](../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Backup_and_Restore/Folder_Structure.md), the **Move or Copy Files** dialog box appears.

6.  If the dialog box appears, click **Move Files**, **Copy Files** (recommended), or **Leave Files**.

    The **Media File** field appears with the path to the file. The [Label field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/label_field.md) appears below it.

7.  In the **Label** field, enter a descriptive label for the sound or movie. For example, you could enter the name of person who spoke the word that was recorded for the particular pronunciation.

> [!TIP]
>
> - *Only* files in an appropriate folder are [backed up](../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Backup_and_Restore/Back_up_this_Project.md) or are shared with [Send/Receive](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Send_Receive/Send_Receive_menu.md). **See Also:** [Linked Files tab](../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Project_Properties/Project_Properties_Linked_Files_tab.md).
>
> - In the **Entry** pane, **Pronunciation** fields are at the [entry level](About_Lex_Edit_fld_levels.md).
>
> - You can [set the field visibility](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/change_the_visibility_of_fields.md) to display or hide the associated fields.
>
> - **See also:** [Audio files overview](../../../Basic_Tasks/Audio_files/Audio_files_overview.md).
