# Insert an example in an extended note

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

See [Extended Note fields overview](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Extended_Note/Extended_Note_fields_overview.md) for an overview of why you might want to insert one or more extended note field sets in your entry.

When you [insert](Insert_an_extended_note.md) an extended note, the **Type**, **Discussion** and **Example** fields appear. The **Translation** field appears after the **Example** field is expanded or it has contents. You can insert more than one example in an extended note. You can insert more than one translation for each example.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, click the **Example** field label that is associated with the desired the **Extended Note** field. Then, do one of the following:

    - If you will use the [Find example sentence](Find_example_sentence.md) command, do these steps:

    <!-- -->

    - 1.  Click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) or right-click the field label, and then click **Insert Example in Note**.

      2.  Click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) or right-click the field label, and then click **Find example** sentence.

    - Enter (type or paste) the example directly in the **Example** field.

4.  In the **Translation** field, enter or edit the translation as needed.

5.  *Optionally,* insert *another* example in the current extended note:

<!-- -->

5.  1.  Click the **Extended Note** field.

    2.  Click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) or right-click the field label, and then click **Insert Example in Note**.

    3.  In the **Example** field, enter or [find](Find_example_sentence.md) an example sentence.

    4.  In the **Translation** field, enter or edit the translation as needed.

        - If necessary, right-click the **Example** field label and then click **Insert Translation** to insert *another* **Translation** field.

<!-- -->

6.  Reorder examples, if an extended note has more than one:\
    Right-click one of them, and then click **Move Example Up** or **Move Example Down**.

> [!TIP]
>
> - If your project has appropriate [audio writing systems](../../../Basic_Tasks/Audio_files/Audio_files_overview.md), you can record or link to an existing audio recording of the example or translation.
>
> - To delete a translation, right-click the **Translation** field label and then click **Delete Translation**.
>
> - You can [insert](Insert_an_extended_note.md) more than one extended note in a sense or subsense.
>
> - You can [configure the dictionary](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to show content from these fields when you configure [Extended Note](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Extended_Note.md).
