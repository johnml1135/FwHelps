# Jump to field

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

In **Lexicon Edit**, the The dictionary preview pane is at the top, above the individual fields. Sometimes it can be difficult to know which field stores a specific part of the entry. In this case, you can use the **Jump to field** option.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** column, click the desired entry.

3.  In the dictionary preview pane, right-click the part of the entry that you want to edit, and then **Jump to field**.

The focus, or "insertion point" of your cursor moves to the [field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/field_descriptions_overview.md) that stores that part of the entry.
