# Enter restrictions

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, if you cannot see the appropriate **Restrictions** field (see ![](../../../assets/images/Important_Icon.gif) **Important** below), [show hidden fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md).

4.  Do one or more of the following:

    - For restrictions at the *entry* level, click the **Restrictions** field that appears above the **Sense** label(s), and then type the restriction(s).

      If you have more than one writing system shown, type the content in the appropriate lines.

    - For restrictions at the *sense* or *subsense* level, click the **Restrictions** field that appears in the applicable sense or subsense, and then enter the restrictions.

      If you have more than one writing system shown, enter the content in the appropriate lines.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - **Restriction** fields appear at the entry-level ([Restrictions](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Restrictions_field.md)), *and* in each sense and subsense ([Restrictions](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/restrictions_field.md)).
>
> - - You can [configure the dictionary](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Configure_Dictionary.md) to display content entered at the sense or subsense level, but *not* at the entry level.
>
> - Each field is a [single-line text field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Field_Types/Single_line_text_field.md). However, the **Restrictions** field in a **Sense** is *multi-Unicode*, not *multi-String*, so it does not store any formatting information, such as [styles](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Format/Styles/Styles_overview.md).
