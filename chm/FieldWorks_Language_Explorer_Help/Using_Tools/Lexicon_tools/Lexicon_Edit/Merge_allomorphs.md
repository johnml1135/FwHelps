# Merge allomorphs

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the entry that has the allomorphs you will merge.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, under **Allomorphs**, click the **Stem Allomorph** or **Affix Allomorph** field label that has the allomorph that you will merge into another allomorph, or into the lexeme form.

    The menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appears.

4.  Click the menu button, and then select **Merge Allomorph into**.

    The **Merge Allomorph** dialog box appears.

5.  In the **Allomorphs** pane, select the allomorph or lexeme form into which you will merge the current allomorph (indicated in the information area at the bottom of the dialog box).

6.  If the description of the merge describes the correct allomorphs or form and the intended merge direction, click **Merge**.

    The form is appended to the form of the receiving allomorph or to the lexeme form. The [Morph Type](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Alternate_Forms_level_flds/Morph_type_fld_allomorph.md) and [Environment(s)](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Alternate_Forms_level_flds/Environments_fld_allomorph.md) of the receiving allomorph or form are retained, but those of the merged allomorph are *not* retained.\
    Further, any interlinearized words that use the allomorph that you merged now use the allomorph or lexeme form into which you merged the allomorph.

7.  Edit the allomorph or lexeme form, if necessary.
