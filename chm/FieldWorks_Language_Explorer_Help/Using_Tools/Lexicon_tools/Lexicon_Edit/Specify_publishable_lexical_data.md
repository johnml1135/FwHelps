# Specify publishable lexical data

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

- Read [What is a publication?](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lists/Publications/What_is_a_Publication.md), and then review the table below.

<table width="100%">
<tbody>
<tr>
<th style="width: 50%"><p>To</p></th>
<th style="width: 50%"><p>Do this</p></th>
</tr>
&#10;<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p><em>Totally</em> exclude an entire lexical <em>entry</em> from a publication (as a headword, all senses, examples, and any references to this entry from elsewhere)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Remove that publication from the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Publish_In_(Publication_Settings).md">Publish Entry In</a> field below <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Publ_Set_level_fields_overview.md">Publication Settings</a>. This <em>overrides</em> other selections, such as in the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_Subentry_under.md">Show Subentry under</a> field, <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_Minor_Entry_Pub_Set_level.md">Show Minor Entry</a> field (entry) or <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Variants_level_fields/Show_Minor_Entry_Var_level.md">Show Minor Entry</a> field (variant).</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Exclude an entry <em>as a main entry headword</em> (it can appear as an indented <a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Subentries.md">subentry headword</a> in a <a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md">root-based layout</a>)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>In the entry you want to exclude, remove the publication from the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_As_Headword_In_field.md">Show As Headword In</a> field.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Exclude a <em>sense</em> or <em>subsense</em> from a publication (as a sense in the lexical entry, and any references to this sense from elsewhere)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Remove that publication from the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Publish_In_(Sense).md">Publish Sense In</a> field in the particular <em>sense</em> or <em>subsense</em>.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Exclude a <em>pronunciation</em> from a publication</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Remove that publication from the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Publish_In_(Pronunciations).md">Publish Pronunciation In</a> field for that <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Pronunciation_field.md">Pronunciation</a> field set.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Exclude a <em>picture</em> from a publication</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Remove that publication from the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Publish_In_(Pictures).md">Publish Picture In</a> field in the particular <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Picture_field.md">Picture</a> field set.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Exclude an <em>example</em> from a publication</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Remove the publication from the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Publish_In_(Example).md">Publish Example In</a> field from that <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/example_field.md">Example</a> field set.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Specify that you want a variant form to be available for display (<a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Main_Minor_entry.md">minor entry</a>)</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Select (<img src="../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG" />) either the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_Minor_Entry_Pub_Set_level.md">Show Minor Entry</a> field below the <strong>Publication Settings</strong> field in the <em>variant</em> entry,</p>
<p><em>or,</em></p>
<p>the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Variants_level_fields/Show_Minor_Entry_Var_level.md">Show Minor Entry</a> field below the <strong>Variants</strong> field in the <em>main</em> entry.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p><a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md">Lexeme-based</a> dictionary layouts:</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><ul>
<li><p>Specify that a complex form <em>that references</em> a component <em>entry</em> should be available for display</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Choose the complex form in the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md">Referenced Complex Forms</a> field below the <strong>Publication Settings</strong> field.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><ul>
<li><p>Specify that a complex form <em>that references</em> a particular <em>sense</em> or <em>subsense</em> should be available for display</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Choose the complex form in the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Referenced_Complex_Forms_(sense).md">Referenced Complex Forms</a> field in the particular <em>sense</em> or <em>subsense</em>.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><ul>
<li><p>Specify that you want a variant form to be available for display (<a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Main_Minor_entry.md">minor entry</a>)</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Select (<img src="../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG" />) either the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_Minor_Entry_Pub_Set_level.md">Show Minor Entry</a> field associated with the variant form below the <strong>Variants</strong> field in the <em>main</em> entry,</p>
<p><em>or,</em></p>
<p>the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_Minor_Entry_Pub_Set_level.md">Show Minor Entry</a> field below the <strong>Publication Settings</strong> field in the <em>variant</em> entry.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><p><a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md">Root-based</a> dictionary layouts:</p></td>
<td style="width: 50%"></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><ul>
<li><p>Specify that you want a complex form entry to appear as an indented subentry under a component entry</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Choose the complex form entry in the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Subentries_(Publication_Settings).md">Subentries</a> field in the component entry.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><ul>
<li><p>Specify that you want a variant form <em>or</em> complex form (could be a complex form that is also a variant) to be available for display (<a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Main_Minor_entry.md">minor entry</a>)</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td style="width: 50%"><p>Select (<img src="../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG" />) the <a href="../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_Minor_Entry_Pub_Set_level.md">Show Minor Entry</a> field below the <strong>Publication Settings</strong> field in the <em>main</em> entry.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%"><a href="../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/About_the_Hybrid_Dictionary_view.md">Hybrid</a> dictionary layouts:</td>

</tr>
