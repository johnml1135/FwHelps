# Choose referenced complex forms

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

(For *Lexeme-based* dictionary [layouts](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md))

When you [choose components](Choose_components_for_Components_field.md) for a complex form, FLEx adds that complex form to the applicable **Referenced Complex Forms** field in each component entry.

You can also remove or add complex forms to the applicable **Referenced Complex Forms** field from the [Complex Forms](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Complex_Forms.md) field (at the entry- *or* sense-level in the complex form entry).

Alternatively, you can do the following:

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** column, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, if you cannot see the desired **Referenced Complex Forms** field, [show hidden fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md).

4.  Click the **Referenced Complex Forms** field. Click the ellipsis button ![](../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG) that appears.

    The **Choose Complex Forms to display in Lexeme-based layouts** dialog box appears. Complex forms that use this entry or sense as a component are listed.

5.  Do any of the following:

    - Clear (![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Menus/Tools/UncheckedBox.PNG)) each complex form you do *not* want available for display.

    - Select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) each complex form you want available for display.

    - Click the **Add a Complex Form** link to open the **Find Lexical Entry** dialog box. Then you can search for an existing entry or create a new entry.

    - Click **OK**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - There is a [Referenced Complex Forms](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md) field below the [Publication Settings](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Publication_Settings_field.md) field, and a [Referenced Complex Forms](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Referenced_Complex_Forms_(sense).md) field in *each* sense and subsense.
>
> In the **Referenced Complex Forms** fields, you can choose which complex forms you want to be available for display in *lexeme-based* dictionary [layouts](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md). You need to [configure](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Using_the_Configure_Dictionary_dialog_box.md) them for display in **Dictionary**.
>
> - For *Root-based* dictionary [layouts](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md), **[Other Referenced Complex Forms](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Other_Referenced_Complex_Forms.md) are those forms that appear in the [Referenced Complex Forms](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Referenced_Complex_Forms_Publication_Settings.md) field, but which are** *not* **also in the [Subentries](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Subentries_(Publication_Settings).md) field.** **See Also:** [Choose subentries for Subentries field](Choose_subentry_for_Subentries_field.md).
