# Choose components for Components field

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

For complex forms (lexeme forms with components), each component may exist as a lexical entry. In this case, you can choose entries or senses in the [Components](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Components_field.md) field. You can also choose a component from the [Show Subentry under](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Publication_Settings_flds/Show_Subentry_under.md) field if the one you need is not listed in the [Choose where to show subentry](Choose_components_for_Show_Subentry_Under.md) dialog box.

If you do not choose components, then the **Complex Form Type** and **Components** field set are deleted when you leave the entry.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, do one of the following:

    - Click the **Components** field, and then click the ellipsis button ![](../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG).

    - Click the **Show Subentry under** field, click the ellipsis button to open the **Choose where to show subentry** dialog box. Click the **Add a Component** link in this dialog box.

      The **Choose Lexical Entry or Sense** dialog box appears. **See** **Tip** below.

4.  In the **Writing System** box, select the writing system of the *form* (lexeme, citation or allomorph) or *gloss* of the desired entry.

5.  In the **Find** box, enter the *form* or *gloss* of the desired entry.

6.  In the **Lexical Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

    If the entry is not listed, do any of the following:

    - Change the spelling in the **Find** box.

    - Select a different writing system in the **Writing System** box.

    - Click **Create** to open the [New Entry](Create_a_lexical_entry.md) dialog box.

7.  In the **Step 2: Select Entry or Sense** pane, do one of the following:

    - Click **Entry**.

    - Click **Specific Sense**, and then select a sense.

8.  Click **OK**.

    The selected component (entry or sense) appears in the **Components** field. If this is the *first* component, it appears in the **Show Subentry under** field.

    Other fields that you need to [specify](Specify_that_Form_is_Complex.md) that a form is complex are added under the [Complex Form Type](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Complex_Form_Type_field.md) field. This is equivalent to clicking **Lexeme Form has components** on the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md) menu button.

    The homograph number will appear, if the selected entry has one. The sense number also appears, if a particular sense was selected.

9.  If needed, [choose components for Show Subentry under field](Choose_components_for_Show_Subentry_Under.md).

> [!TIP]
>
> - The **Choose Lexical Entry or Sense** dialog box also appears when you [create a new complex entry while analyzing](../../Texts_%26_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Create_new_lex_entry_interlinear_view.md) words in a text. Then you can choose the first component while the window shows interlinear text.
