# Enter bibliographical information

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, if you cannot see the desired **Bibliography** field, [show hidden fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md).

4.  Do one or more of the following:

    - For bibliographical information at the *entry* level, click the **Bibliography** field that appears above the **Sense** label(s), and then type the information.

    - For bibliographical information at the *sense* or *subsense* level, click the **Bibliography** field that appears in the applicable sense or subsense, and then type the information.
