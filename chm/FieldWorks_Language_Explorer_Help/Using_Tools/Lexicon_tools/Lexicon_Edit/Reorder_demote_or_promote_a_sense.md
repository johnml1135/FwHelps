# Reorder, demote or promote a sense

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, click the **Sense** (sense or subsense) field label that you will move, promote or demote.

    Links and a menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appear.

4.  Click the menu button, and then click one of the following commands:

    (The available menu commands depend on the item's position in the hierarchical structure and number of senses in the entry.)

    - **Move Sense Up**

    - **Move Sense Down**

    - **Demote**

    - **Promote**

    The current sense or subsense moves vertically or hierarchically within the entry. You can see the change also in the [dictionary preview](../../../User_Interface/Menus/View/View_overview.md) pane. Alternatively, you can [move a sense](Move_sense_to_new_entry.md) to a *new* (automatically created) entry *or* you can [drag and drop](Move_sense_to_another_entry.md) a sense into a different *existing* entry.

> [!TIP]
>
> - You can move senses up or down to change their order within an entry. You can also promote subsenses and demote senses in the hierarchical structure.
>
> - Sense labels are numbered, for example as **Sense 1**, **Sense 2**, and so on. Subsense labels are **Sense 1.1**, **Sense 1.2**, and then **Sense 2.1**, **Sense 2.2**, and so on.
