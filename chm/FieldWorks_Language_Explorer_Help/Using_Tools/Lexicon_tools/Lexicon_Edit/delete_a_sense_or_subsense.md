# Delete a sense or subsense

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

This topic helps you delete one sense or subsense from one entry. To delete multiple senses at the same time, see [Bulk delete entries or sense](../Bulk_Edit_Entries/Bulk_Delete_Entries_Senses.md).

Except for [variants](../../Lists_tools/About_Variants.md), lexical entries must have at least *one* sense. For variants, you can delete the only sense, which may be important if you use a [parser](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Parser/Parsing_words_overview.md).

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the entry that has the sense or subsense you will delete.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, click any field in the sense or subsense you want to delete.

    Links and a menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appear.

4.  Click the menu button, and then click **Delete this Sense and any SubSenses** if it is available.

    The **Delete Sense** warning box appears.

5.  If you are sure you want to delete the sense or subsense and *any subordinate* subsenses identified in the warning box, click **Delete**.

    The sense or subsense, and any subordinate subsenses are deleted from the entry. In addition, if the associated grammatical information is *not used* with another sense or subsense, or in any [analyses](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md), then that grammatical information is deleted from the **Grammatical Info Details** area.
