# Choose a Stem Allomorph Label

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

The topic [Insert a Stem Allomorph Label](../../Grammar_tools/Category_Edit/Insert_a_Stem_Allomorph_Label.md) has important information about Stem Allomorph Labels.

In **Lexicon Edit**:

- A **Stem Allomorph Label** [field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Alternate_Forms_level_flds/Stem_Allomorph_Label_fld_Allomorph.md) appears under **Allomorphs** (for stem allomorphs)

- A **Stem Allomorph Label** [field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/stem_allomorph_label_field_lex_form.md) appears under the **Lexeme Form** field (for non-affix [Morph Types](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Morph_Type_Field.md)).

- A **From** **Stem Allomorph Label** [field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Grammatical_Info_Details_fields/From_Stem_Allomorph_Label_field.md) appears under **Grammatical Info Details** (for derivational affixes).

Use the appropriate field when you choose a Stem Allomorph Label.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** column, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, if you cannot see the appropriate **Stem Allomorph Label** or **From Stem Allomorph Label** field, [show hidden fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md).

4.  Click the appropriate field, and then click the ellipsis button ![](../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG) that appears.

    The **Choose Stem **Allomorph Label**** dialog box appears.

5.  Do any of these steps:

    - Select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) **Display usage figures** to see how many times each **Stem **Allomorph Label**** is used in the project.

    - Click the **Edit the Stem **Allomorph Label** for categories** link to open the **Status** list. There you can [insert a Stem Allomorph Label](../../Grammar_tools/Category_Edit/Insert_a_Stem_Allomorph_Label.md) or edit one.

    - Click a stem allomorph label or **\<Empty\>** (to clear the field). Click **OK**.
