# Move a sense to another entry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

Using *two* open windows, you can use *drag and drop* to move a sense or subsense to a different lexical entry. Alternatively, you can [move a sense to a new entry](Move_sense_to_new_entry.md) (automatically created).

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  On the **Window** menu, click **New Window**, and then do the following:

    - In one window, select the *source* entry (has the sense or subsense you will move).

    - In the other window, select the *destination* entry (where you will move the sense or subsense).

    - Reposition and resize the two windows until you can see the sense you will move in the source entry, and see an existing sense in the destination entry.

    - If the destination entry does *not* currently have a sense, [insert a sense](Insert_a_sense_or_subsense_in_an_entry.md) (temporarily).

    - If you cannot see the **Gloss** fields, select [Show Hidden Fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md).

3.  In the source **Entry** pane, click and hold the mouse button on the **Gloss** field label for the sense or subsense you will move. Drag the mouse pointer near a **Gloss** field label of an existing sense in the destination entry.

    When the mouse pointer is over an *invalid* drop location, it appears as a circle with a line through it. When the mouse pointer is over a *valid* drop location, it changes back to your normal pointer, but with a small rectangle indicating there is content waiting to be dropped.

4.  Release the mouse button.

    The sense or subsense is moved from the source to the destination entry.

5.  If you inserted a temporary sense in the destination entry, [delete](delete_a_sense_or_subsense.md) that sense.

> [!TIP]
>
> - It is not critical where you drop the sense in the destination entry as you can subsequently [reorder or promote/demote](Reorder_demote_or_promote_a_sense.md) the sense or subsense within the entry.
>
> - If you moved the *only* sense from the source entry, you probably should [insert a sense](Insert_a_sense_or_subsense_in_an_entry.md) to make sure it has at least one sense, if the entry requires a sense.
