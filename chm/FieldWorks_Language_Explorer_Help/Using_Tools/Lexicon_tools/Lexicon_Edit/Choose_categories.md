# Choose categories for Attaches to Categories field

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

The [Attaches to Categories](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Grammatical_Info_Details_fields/attaches_to_categories_field.md) field appears in lexical entries that are set to *enclitic* or *proclitic* in the [Morph Type](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Morph_Type_Field.md) field ([entry](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Entry_level_fields_overview.md) level) or in the [Morph Type](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Alternate_Forms_level_flds/Morph_type_fld_allomorph.md) field for one or more of the allomorphs.

In an **Attaches to Categories** field, you choose one or more categories (parts of speech) to which the enclitic or proclitic may attach.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** column, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, if you cannot see the **Attaches to Category** field, [show hidden fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md).

4.  Click the **Attaches to Category** field under **Grammatical Info Details**.

    An ellipsis button ![](../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG) appears.

5.  Click the ellipsis button.

    The **Choose** **Attaches to Categories** dialog box appears.

6.  Do any of the following:

    - Select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) the **Display usage figures** to see how many times each grammatical category is used in the project.

    - Click the **Add a category** link to [insert or edit](../../Grammar_tools/Category_Edit/Insert_a_Category.md) a grammatical category.

    - Select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) the categories you want to display. Click **OK**.
