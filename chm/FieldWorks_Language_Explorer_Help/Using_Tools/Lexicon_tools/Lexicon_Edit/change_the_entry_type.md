# Change the entry type

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, do *any* of the following:

    - On the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md), click the menu button (![PIC](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG)), then click one of the following commands:

    - **Lexeme Form is a variant** to add the [Variant Type](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Variant_Type_field.md) and related fields.

      **Lexeme Form has components** to add the [Complex Form Type](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Complex_Form_Type_field.md) and related fields.

    - Right-click a **Variant Type** field, and click ![PIC](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/DeleteXred.png) **Delete Variant Info**.\
      The **Variant Type** and related fields are deleted from the entry.\
      Repeat this step if the entry has [another](Add_another_Variant_Info_section.md) **Variant Info** section.

    <!-- -->

    - Right-click a **Complex Form Type** field, and click ![PIC](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/DeleteXred.png) **Delete Complex Form Info**.\
      The **Complex Form Type** and related fields are deleted from the entry.

4.  If you added fields, do *either* of the following:

    - [Choose a Variant Type](Choose_variant_types.md) and [choose Variant of entry or sense](Choose_Variant_of_entry.md).

    - [Choose a Complex Form Type](Choose_Complex_Form_Type.md) and [choose components](Choose_components_for_Components_field.md).

5.  Do *any* of the steps in these topics:

    - [Specify that a form is a variant](Specify_that_a_form_is_a_variant.md)

    - [Specify that a form is complex](Specify_that_Form_is_Complex.md)

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - To prevent partially-defined entries, **Unspecified Variant** or **Unspecified Complex Form** appears until you choose a specific type.
>
> - **See:** [Main Entry and Minor Entry](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Main_Minor_entry.md) and [Dictionary layouts](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md).
