# Choose Variant of entry or sense

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired variant entry.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, if you cannot see the [Variant of](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Variant_of_field.md) field, [show hidden fields](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_Hidden_Fields.md) or [add another Variant Info section](Add_another_Variant_Info_section.md).

4.  Click the **Variant of** field, and then click the ellipsis button ![](../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG) that appears.

    The **Choose Lexical Entry or Sense** dialog box appears.

5.  In the **Writing System** box, select the writing system of the *form* (lexeme, citation or allomorph) or *gloss* of the entry you want to find.

6.  In the **Find** box, enter the form or gloss of the entry you want to find.

7.  In the **Lexical Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

    If the desired entry is not listed, do any if the following:

    - Change the spelling in the **Find** box.

    - Select a different writing system in the **Writing System** box.

    - Click **Create** to open the [New Entry](Create_a_lexical_entry.md) dialog box.

8.  In the **Step 2: Select Entry or Sense** pane, do one of the following:

    - Click **Entry**.

    - Click **Specific Sense**, and then select a sense.

9.  Click **OK**.

    The selected entry or sense appears in the **Variant of** field. Also, in the entry you selected, the entry appears in one of these fields: If you selected the entry, it appears in a [Variant Form](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Variants_level_fields/Variant_Form_field.md) field; If you selected a sense of the entry, it appears in a [Variant of Sense](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Variant_of_Sense_field.md) field.

    Other fields that you need to [specify](Specify_that_a_form_is_a_variant.md) that the form is a variant are added under the [Variant Type](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Variant_Type_field.md) field. This is equivalent to clicking **Lexeme Form is a variant** on the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md) menu button menu.

    The homograph number will appear, if the selected entry has one. The sense number also appears, if a particular sense was selected.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> As discussed in [Insert a variant form](Insert_a_variant_form.md), you insert a variant form in a lexical entry as a variant of that entry. You can display the variant form as an entry in the **Entry** pane.
>
> Then, for each variant form that is related to *multiple* lexical entries, you have these options:
>
> - You can choose additional entries in an existing **Variants of** field.
>
> - You can insert an additional set of variant-related fields for cases when you need to choose a *different* variant type, **Show Minor Entry** field selection or summary.
>
> If you do not choose an entry or sense for the **Variant of** field, then the **Variant Type** and **Variant of** field set are deleted when you leave the entry.
