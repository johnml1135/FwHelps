# Insert a sense or subsense in an entry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the desired entry.

3.  To insert a sense or subsense*,* click an *existing sense* (or maybe an *existing subsense*) and then do one of the following:

    - Click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that appears next to the **Sense** field (**Sense 1**, **Sense 1.1**, **Sense 2** and so on), and then click **Insert Sense** *or* **Insert SubSense**.

    - Click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG) on the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md), and then click **Insert Sense** *or* **Insert Subsense (in sense)**.

    - On the **Insert** menu, click **Sense** *or* **Subsense (in sense)**.

    - Click the **Insert Sense** link that appears to the right of the **Sense** field. (There is no link for subsenses.)

    A *sense* is inserted into the current entry or a *subsense* is inserted into the existing sense or subsense you clicked.

> [!TIP]
>
> - You can insert subsenses in subsenses. The sense/subsense numbers indicate the levels, such as **Sense 1**, **Sense 1.1**, **Sense 1.1.1**, **Sense 1.1.1.1** and so on. However, it likely becomes nonsensical to create a deep hierarchy with senses.
>
> - Currently, [field visibility](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/Show_hiding_flds_oview.md) settings are separate for each level of nesting. If you use the subsenses feature, you may want to *manually* keep all the levels in sync whenever configuring the visibility of a given sense field. For example, if you set **Grammar Note** to **Always visible** under senses, also find or create a subsense and do the same, and so on for any deeper levels you actually use.
>
> - You can do any of the following:
>
> - - [Add a new sense](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/add_a_new_sense.md) (while [analyzing](../../Texts_%26_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Analyze_Text_overview.md) a text)
>
>   - [Move a sense or subsense into another entry](Move_sense_to_another_entry.md)
>
>   - [Move a sense or subsense into a new (automatically created) entry](Move_sense_to_new_entry.md)
>
>   - [Reorder, demote or promote a sense or subsense](Reorder_demote_or_promote_a_sense.md).
>
> - If the entry currently does not have any existing sense, use the **Insert** menu or Information bar to insert the first sense.
