# Delete a variant form

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

## Delete a variant form reference (from its "variant of" entry)

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the "variant of" entry that has the unwanted variant form in its **Variants** area.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, click the **Variant Form** field for the variant you want to delete, and then do one of the following:

    - Click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that appears, and then select **Delete Reference**.

    - Right-click the **Variant Form** field label, and then select **Delete Reference**.

    The **Variant Form** field and its associated fields are removed from the **Variants** area. The reference to the variant entry is deleted, but you can still find the variant entry in the **Entries** pane.

## Delete the variant form (from its "variant of" entry)

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the "variant of" entry (that is, the entry that has the unwanted variant form in its **Variants** area).

3.  Click the **Variant Form** field for the variant you want to delete, and then do one of the following:

    - Click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that appears, and then select **Delete Variant**.

    - Right-click the **Variant Form** field label, and then select **Delete Variant**.

    The **Delete Entry** warning box appears. The deletion may affect [interlinearized texts](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Analyze_Text_overview.md). In this case, the warning box information includes how many [analyses](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md) reference the entry.

4.  Click **Delete**.

    The variant entry is removed from the displayed **Variants** area and from the **Entries** pane.

## Delete the variant form (from the Entries pane)

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the variant form you will delete.

3.  Do one of the following:

    - On the [Standard](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Standard_toolbar.md) toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/RedDeleteRecordX.GIF).

    - Right-click the entry (in the **Entries** pane), and then select **Delete selected Entry**.

    - On the **Edit** menu, click **Delete Entry**.

    The **Delete Entry** warning box appears. The deletion may affect [interlinearized texts](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Analyze_Text_overview.md). In this case, the warning box information includes how many [analyses](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md) reference the entry.

4.  Click **Delete**.

    The variant entry is removed from the displayed **Entries** pane and the **Variants** area in the "Variant of" entry.
