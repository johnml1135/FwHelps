# Swap Lexeme Form with Allomorph

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Lexicon Edit*

You can exchange (swap) a lexeme form with an allomorph. All the associated fields move. Either can be an affix process or a regular allomorph.

Any analyses (in [Word Analyses](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md), [Interlinear Texts](../../Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/texts_edit_overview.md) and so on) and fields ([Components](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Components_field.md), [Variant of](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Variant_of_field.md) and so on) that use the lexeme form or allomorph change when you swap the forms.

## From the Lexeme Form field

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the entry that has the forms you will swap.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, click the **Lexeme Form** field label or the lexeme form.

    A menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appears.

4.  Do one of the following:

    - Click the menu button, and then click **Swap Lexeme Form with Allomorph**.

    - Right-click the field label *or* lexeme form, and then click **Swap Lexeme Form with Allomorph**.

    The **Swap Lexeme Form with Allomorph** dialog box appears.

5.  In the **Allomorphs** pane, select the desired allomorph.

6.  Click **Swap**.

## From an Allomorph field

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Lexicon Edit**.

2.  In the **Entries** pane, click the entry that has the forms you will swap.

3.  In the **Entry** pane, click the **Stem Allomorph** or **Affix Allomorph** field label for the allomorph that you want to swap with lexeme form.

    A menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appears.

4.  Click the menu button, and then click **Swap Allomorph with Lexeme Form**.
