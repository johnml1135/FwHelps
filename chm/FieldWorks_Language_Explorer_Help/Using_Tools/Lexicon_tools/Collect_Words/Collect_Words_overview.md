# Collect Words overview

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Collect Words*

In **Collect Words**, you rapidly [create lexical entries](Create_a_Lexical_entry_in_Collect_Words.md) that are categorized (classified) by [semantic domains](../../Lists_tools/List_item_usage_table.md).

As a general overview, you select a semantic domain, and then enter vernacular words with glosses or [initial definitions](Collect_Words_initial_definition_considerations.md). Each semantic domain provides questions and examples to help elicit words. Later, you will need to complete the analysis of the each word, develop *quality* definitions, and so on.

You can also [export Semantic Domain worksheets](../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Export/Export_Semantic_Domain_Worksheets.md) and print them for use with a *Rapid Word Collection* workshop.

## **Semantic Domains** pane (*center*)

- You can expand (![](../../../assets/images/Plus_sign_box.GIF)) the hierarchical list and [search](Search_Semantic_Domains.md) (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Collect_Words/MagnifyingGlass.png)) for a semantic domain.

- The number in parenthesis is the number of existing lexical entries classified with the semantic domain.

## **Semantic Domain** pane (*right*)

- The *top* portion displays questions and related information.

  - You can [enter](enter_the_vernacular_name_of_a_semantic_domain.md) the vernacular name of a semantic domain.

  - You can [insert](../../Lists_tools/Insert_a_Question_Semantic_Domain.md) a question.

- The *bottom* portion displays columns and rows.

  - You can [configure columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Configure_Columns_overview.md) to specify *word forms* (citation or lexeme) and *meaning* (gloss or definition), or other columns, their associated writing systems (if permitted), and their order in the display.

See Also: [Collect Words with Dialect Labels](Collect_Words_with_Dialect_Labels.md).

1.  - You can type in a row with a white background to [create](Create_a_Lexical_entry_in_Collect_Words.md) a lexical entry.

See Also: [Initial definition considerations](Collect_Words_initial_definition_considerations.md).

- You can *right-click* a darkened row and then click **Show Entry In Lexicon** to display the entry in **Lexicon Edit**.

Darkened backgrounds indicate *existing* entries that are no longer editable here. You can edit them in **Lexicon Edit**.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - Misspelled words are *not* [flagged](../../../Basic_Tasks/Spell_Checking/Spell_checking_overview.md), currently, in **Collect Words**.
>
> Find and correct spelling errors elsewhere, such as in **Lexicon Edit**. For example, you can [filter](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filter_data.md) the **Glosses** column to show only entries that have spelling errors. Then you can edit words directly in the column or directly in the field.
>
> - Parenthesis "( )" around content in a cell indicates that the column is displaying content from another field.
>
> For example, suppose an entry had only a lexeme form. If you displayed the **Word (Citation Form)** column in **Collect Words**, but not the **Word (Lexeme Form)** column, the lexeme form would appear in parenthesis in the **Word (Citation Form)** column.
>
> - You can change columns widths with your mouse.
