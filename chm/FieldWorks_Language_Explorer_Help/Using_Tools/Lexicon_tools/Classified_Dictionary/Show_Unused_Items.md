# Show Unused Items

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Classified Dictionary*

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Classified Dictionary**.

2.  On the **Classified Senses** [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md), do one of the following:

<!-- -->

1.  - *Clear* **Show Unused Items** to show *only* *used* semantic domains (semantic domains that currently classify at least one sense or subsense of a lexical entry).

    - *Select* **Show Unused Items** to show *all* semantic domains, including the unused ones.
