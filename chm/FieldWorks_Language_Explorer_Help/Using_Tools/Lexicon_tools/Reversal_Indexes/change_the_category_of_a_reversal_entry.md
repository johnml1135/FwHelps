# Change the reversal category of a reversal entry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Reversal Indexes*

Do the following steps in **Reversal Indexes**:

1.  In the center pane, click the reversal entry or subentry

2.  In the **Reversal Entry** pane, click the **Reversal Category** field down arrow, and then click one of the following:

    - a category from the list

    - **\<Not Sure\>**

    - **More** to [open](../Lexicon_Edit/Using_Add_from_Catalog_dialog_box.md) the **Add from Catalog** dialog box. Click the desired category, and then click **Add**.

The category is added to the **Reversal Category** field.

If you chose one from the catalog, that category is added to the [Reversal Index Categories (Lists)](../../Lists_tools/about_reversal_index_categories.md). You may need to manually [edit the list item](../../Lists_tools/Edit_a_list_item_or_subitem.md) to include names and information in other writing systems for that category.
