# Insert a reversal entry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Reversal Indexes*

[About Reversal Entries](../Lexicon_Edit/About_Reversal_Entries.md) and [Enter a reversal entry](../Lexicon_Edit/Enter_a_reversal_entry.md) discuss another way to enter a reversal entry and subentry.

To insert a reversal entry from the **Reversal Indexes** area, do the following:

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Reversal Indexes**.

2.  Click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG) ([Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md)), and then select the reversal index in which you will insert a reversal entry.

3.  Do one of the following:

    - On the [Insert toolbar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Insert_toolbar.md), click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Create_Reversal_Entry.GIF).

    - On the **Insert** menu, click **Reversal Entry**.

4.  In the **Reversal Entry** pane, do the following:

    - In the **Reversal Form** field, type the form.

    - In the **Reversal Category** field, [choose the category](change_the_category_of_a_reversal_entry.md) for the reversal entry.

    - In the **Referenced Senses** field, [add a sense to the reversal entry](Add_a_sense_to_a_reversal_entry.md).
