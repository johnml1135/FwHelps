# Delete a reversal entry or subentry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Reversal Indexes*

Do the following steps in **Reversal Indexes**:

1.  In the center pane, click the reversal entry you will delete or which has the subentry you will delete.

2.  In the **Reversal Entry** pane, click the [Reversal Form field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Reversal_Indexes_fields/form_field.md) for the entry *or* subentry you will delete.

Then, do one of the following:

- Click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that is on the left side of the applicable **Reversal Form** field label (for the entry or a *particular* subentry), and then click **Delete this Entry and any Subentries**.

- To entirely delete a reversal entry *and any subentries*,

on the **Edit** menu, click **Delete Reversal Entry**, or\
on the [Standard](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Standard_toolbar.md) toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/RedDeleteRecordX.GIF).

The **Delete Reversal Entry** warning box appears with the form of the reversal entry *or* subentry that will be deleted.

It does *not* inform you if it has any subentries.

3.  Click **Delete**.

The reversal form and any of its subentries are deleted from the **Reversal Indexes** area *and* from any associated lexical entries.

###  **Tip:**

- You cannot use **Undo** (`Ctrl+Z`) to restore a deleted entry or subentry. For the most control over what you will delete, use the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that is next to the applicable **Form** field (for the entry or a *particular* subentry).
