# Insert a reversal subentry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Reversal Indexes*

Do the following steps in **Reversal Indexes**:

1.  In the center pane, click the reversal entry that will have the reversal subentry.

2.  In the **Reversal Entry** pane (*right*), click the **Subentries** field label, and then do one of the following:

    - Click the **Insert Reversal Subentry** link that appears.

    - Click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) that appears, and then click **Insert Reversal Subentry**.

3.  Do the following in the newly inserted subentry:

    - In the **Reversal Form** field, type the form.

    - In the **Reversal Category** field, [choose the category](change_the_category_of_a_reversal_entry.md) for the reversal subentry.

    - In the **Referenced Senses** field, [add a sense to a reversal subentry](Add_a_sense_to_a_reversal_entry.md).

> [!TIP]
>
> - [Enter a reversal entry](../Lexicon_Edit/Enter_a_reversal_entry.md) discusses another way to enter a reversal subentry from **Lexicon Edit**.
