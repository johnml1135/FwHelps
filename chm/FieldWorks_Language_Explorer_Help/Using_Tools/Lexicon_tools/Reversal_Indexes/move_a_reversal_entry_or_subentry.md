# Move a reversal entry or subentry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Reversal Indexes*

You can move a reversal entry or subentry into another reversal entry or subentry. This is different from [merging](Merge_a_reversal_entry_or_subentry.md). What is *moved is added as a subentry of the receiving entry or subentry*.

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Reversal Indexes**.

2.  In the **Reversal Index** pane, do the following:

    - On the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md), click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG), and then select the writing that has the reversal entry or subentry that you will move.

    - Click the reversal entry or subentry that you will move.

3.  In the **Reversal Entry** pane, do the following:

    - Click the **Reversal Form** field for reversal entry or subentry you will move.

      A menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appears.

    - Click the menu button, and then select **Move Entry**.

      The **Move Reversal Entry** dialog box appears.

4.  In the dialog box, do the following:

    - In the **Find** box, enter the form of the reversal entry into which you want to move the current reversal entry.

    - In the **Reversal Entries** pane, select the reversal entry into which you want to merge the current reversal entry.

    - Click **Move Entry**.

    The reversal entry is moved.
