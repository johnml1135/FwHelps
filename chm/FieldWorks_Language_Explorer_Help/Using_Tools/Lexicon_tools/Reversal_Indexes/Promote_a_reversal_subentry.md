# Promote a reversal subentry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Reversal Indexes*

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Lexicon**, and then click **Reversal Indexes**.

2.  In the **Reversal Index** pane, do the following:

    - On the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md), click the menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG), and then select the writing system that has the reversal subentry you will promote.

    - Click the reversal subentry you will promote.

3.  In the **Reversal Entry** pane, do the following:

    - Right-click the **Reversal Form** field for reversal subentry you will promote, and then click **Promote**.

      The selected subentry is promoted one level up (to the left in the hierarchical list of reversal subentries (or sub-subentries).

    - Repeat this step, if you need to promote the subentry another level.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - You *cannot* demote a reversal entry.
