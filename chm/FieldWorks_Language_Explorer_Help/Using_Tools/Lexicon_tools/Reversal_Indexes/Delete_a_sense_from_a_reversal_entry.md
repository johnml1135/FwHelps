# Delete a sense from a Reversal Entry

*Using Tools › Lexicon tools › Reversal Indexes*

Do the following in **Reversal Indexes**:

1.  In the center pane, click the reversal entry or subentry that has the sense you will delete.

2.  In the **Referenced Senses** [field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Reversal_Indexes_fields/senses_field.md), click the sense.

Then press `Delete` (or `Backspace`) on your keyboard.
