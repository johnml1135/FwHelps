# Choose time of event

*Using Tools › Notebook › Record Edit*

In addition to selecting the [date of event](Set_date_of_event.md), you can also choose the time of day when the event occurred.

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Notebook**, and then click **Record Edit**.

2.  In the **Records** pane, click the record you want to change.

3.  In the **Record** pane, click the **Time of Event** field, and then click the ellipsis button ![](../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG) that appears.

    The **Choose Time of Event** dialog box appears.

4.  Do any of these steps:

    - Click the ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Field_Types/Plus_sign_box.GIF) (plus sign) to expand an item, if necessary.

    - Select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) the **Display usage figures** to see how many times each **Time of Day** list item is used in the project.

    - Click the **Edit the Time of Day list** link to [insert](../../Lists_tools/Insert_a_list_item.md) or [edit](../../Lists_tools/Edit_a_list_item_or_subitem.md) a **Time of Day** list item or [subitem](../../Lists_tools/Insert_a_list_subitem.md).

    - Select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) the items you want to display. Click **OK**.
