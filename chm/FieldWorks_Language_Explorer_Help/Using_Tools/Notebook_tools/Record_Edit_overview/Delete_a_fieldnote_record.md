# Delete a fieldnote record or subrecord

*Using Tools › Notebook › Record Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Notebook**, and then click **Record Edit** or **Browse**.

2.  In the **Records** pane, click the record or subrecord you want to delete.

3.  Do one of the following:

    - On the [Standard](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Standard_toolbar.md) toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/RedDeleteRecordX.GIF).

    - On the **Edit** menu, click **Delete Record**.

    - Right-click the record in a columnar view, and then click **Delete selected Record** on the menu that appears.

    A **Delete** **Record** information box appears. It identifies the current selection, and asks if you want to continue with the deletion. *It does not distinguish between a record and subrecord.* If you clicked a record, all of its subrecords are also deleted.

4.  If you are sure you want to delete the fieldnote record or subrecord identified in the information box, click **Delete**.

    The selected fieldnote record and any of its subrecords are deleted, *or* the particular subrecord is deleted. If the record has content in one or more [Text](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Text_field_(Notebook).md) field, that text is *not* deleted from the **Texts & Words** area.

    - Otherwise, click **Cancel**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - It is recommended that you consider [backing up](../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Backup_and_Restore/Back_up_this_Project.md) your the project *before* you delete anything that you might need in the future.
