# Reorder, demote or promote a record

*Using Tools › Notebook › Record Edit*

- In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Notebook**, and then click **Record Edit**.

## Demote a record to become a subrecord

1.  In the **Record** pane, click the record you want to demote.

2.  Click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG)) that is on the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md), next to the **Record** label, and then click **Demote Record**.

The **Choose Owner of Demoted Record** dialog box appears.

3.  In the dialog box, click the record into which you want to demote the record, and then click **OK**.

    - Optionally, you can select (![](../../../assets/images/CheckedBox.PNG)) **Display usage figures** to see how many times the records listed in the dialog box have been used as cross references, such as in for [supporting evidence](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Support_Evid_field_Ntbk.md), [superseded by](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Superceded_By_field_Ntbk.md) and so on.

## Move (reorder), or promote or demote subrecords

1.  In the **Record** pane, click the [heading field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Subrecords_field.md), such as **Observation**, for the subrecord or sub record of a subrecord that you will move, promote or demote.

    Links and a menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appear.

2.  Click the menu button, and then click one of the following commands:

    (The available menu commands depend on the item's position in the hierarchical structure and number of senses in the entry.)

    - **Move Up**

    - **Move Down**

    - **Demote**

    - **Promote**

    The current subrecord, or subrecord of a subrecord, moves

    - one position vertically (up or down) to change the order,

    - one position hierarchically (promoted or demoted) within the record, or

    - from a subrecord to become a top-level record. If you promote a subrecord, it becomes a record. You see this in the `Title` column as the title now appears in bold font.

> [!TIP]
>
> - *Subrecords* are numbered, for example as **1 Event**, **2 Observation**, and so on. *Subrecords of subrecords* are numbered, for example as **1.1 Event**, **1.2 Observation**, and then **2.1 Event**,**2.2 Observation**, and so on. The hierarchy can go deeper, such as **1.1.1. Observation** and so on.
>
> - You can move subrecords, and subrecords of subrecords, up or down to change their order within a record. You can promote or demote any of these in the hierarchical structure.
