# Enter external materials

*Using Tools › Notebook › Record Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Notebook**, and then click **Record Edit**.

2.  In the **Records** pane, click the record you want to change.

3.  In the **Record** pane, click the **External Materials** field.

4.  If the thing that you want to refer to is *not* available to access with a hyperlink, then type the information about its existence and location.

5.  If you can refer to the materials with a hyperlink, do any of the following:

    - Type the text you want to use as the hyperlink.

    - Insert or paste a [hyperlink](../../../Basic_Tasks/Creating_Hyperlinks/Creating_Hyperlinks_overview.md).

    - [Apply a style](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Format/apply_a_style_to_text.md) (*character* style, in most text fields; *paragraph* style, in multiparagraph text fields).

> [!NOTE]
>
> Use the [External Materials](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/External_Materials_Ntbk.md) field to reference materials that are related to the current record, but that are outside (external) of the FieldWorks project.
>
> - You can insert hyperlinks those materials (pictures, sound files, and so on) if they are accessible to your computer.
>
> - You can also document the existence and location of things that are not stored in or accessible to your computer (artifacts, ruins, recordings, artwork, printed information, and so on).
