# Insert a fieldnote subrecord

*Using Tools › Notebook › Record Edit*

In **Notebook**, you can insert one or more fieldnote subrecords in an existing record. You can also insert one or more subrecords to other subrecords.

- In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Notebook**, and then click **Record Edit**.

## Insert a subrecord of a record

1.  In the **Records** pane, click the record into which you want to insert a subrecord.

2.  Do one of the following:

    - Click or right-click the [Subrecords](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Subrecords_field.md) field label, and then click the **Insert Subrecord** link that appears.

    - In the right pane, click the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md) menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG), and then select **Insert Subrecord**.

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Insert/Insert_overview.md) menu, click **Subrecord**.

    The **New Record** dialog box appears.

3.  In the dialog box, do these steps:

    - Enter a title for the record.

    - [Choose](Choose_record_type.md) the record type.

    - Click **Create**.

    A new subrecord appears. Subrecords are numbered (**1.**, **2.**, and so on). The **Subrecord of** [column](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Configure_Columns_overview.md) displays the numbers. It is ready for you to [enter data](Record_Edit_overview.md). You can [change the record type](Choose_record_type.md) later, if necessary.

## Insert a subrecord of a subrecord

1.  In the **Records** pane, click the subrecord into which you want to insert a subrecord. Alternatively, click the record that has the subrecord into which you will insert a subrecord.

2.  In the right pane, click the subrecord into which you will insert the subrecord.

3.  Do one of these steps:

    - Click or right-click the numbered header label for the subrecord, and then click the **Insert Subrecord of subrecord**.

    - In the right pane, click the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md) menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Information_bar_Menu_button.PNG), and then select **Insert Subrecord of subrecord**.

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Insert/Insert_overview.md) menu, click **Subrecord of subrecord**.

    The **New Record** dialog box appears.

4.  In the dialog box, do the following

    - Enter a title for the record.

    - Select a record type.

    - Click **Create**.

    A new subrecord appears. Subrecords of subrecords are numbered (**1.1.**, **1.2.**, **2.1.**, and so on). The **Subrecord of** column displays the number. It is ready for you to [enter data](Record_Edit_overview.md). You can [change the record type](Choose_record_type.md) later, if necessary, you can also [reorder, demote or promote a record](Reorder_demote_or_promote_a_record.md) or subrecord.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - Different records types have different [default fields](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Notebook/Notebook_fields_overview.md) to support their different purposes. However, you can change the [visibility](../../../Basic_Tasks/Showing_and_hiding_fields/change_the_visibility_of_fields.md) for each field individually.
>
>   - "Event" records (**Observation**, **Performance**, **Interview**, and so on) consist of information that illustrates cultural behavior or other aspects of community life.
>
>   - **Analysis** record consists of a proposed explanation of an observed cultural behavior for further study. Analysis records allow you to describe your research agenda and link to event records that support or counter each hypothesis.
