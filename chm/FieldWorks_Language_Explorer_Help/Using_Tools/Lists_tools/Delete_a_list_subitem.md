# Delete a list subitem

*Using Tools › Lists tools*

Here the term *item* includes category, type, domain and so on. Similarly, the term *subitem* includes subcategory, subtype, subdomain and so on. Accordingly, the menu commands might be worded differently, but the steps are the same.

It is recommended that you [back up](../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Backup_and_Restore/Back_up_this_Project.md) the project before you delete any **Lists** area list items that may be used throughout the project.

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Lists**, and then click the list from which you will delete a list subitem.

2.  Do *one* of the following:

    - In the center pane, expand the list item to expose the subitem you will delete, and then click that subitem. On the **Edit** menu, click **Delete List Item**, *or* on the [Standard](../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Standard_toolbar.md) toolbar, click ![](../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lists_tools/RedDeleteRecordX.GIF).

    - In the center pane, expand the list item to expose the item or subitem under which the subitem you want to delete resides, and then select that item or subitem. Then, in the right pane, under the **Subitems** (or **Subcategories** or **Subdomains**) label, right-click the subitem you want to delete, and then click **Delete subitem and its subitems** in the menu at appears.

      (Alternatively, click the subitem, and then click the menu button ![](../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lists_tools/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that appears to access the **Delete subitem and its subitems** menu command. Click that menu command.)

    - For the **Reversal Index Category** list, the center pane is a browse-style display with columns that, currently, does not show the subcategories. Therefore, in the **Categories (or Parts or Speech)** pane, click the parent category. In the **Category (or Part of Speech)** pane, click the [Name field](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lists/Reversal_Index_Categories_fields/Name_field_Reversal_Index_Categories.md) for the subcategory you want to delete, and then click the menu button ![](../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Lists_tools/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) that appears. Click **Delete this category and any subcategories** on the menu that appears.

    The **Delete Category (or Part of Speech)** warning box appears.

3.  If you are sure you want to delete the subitem specified in the warning box and any subitems, click **Delete**.

    That subitem and any subordinate subitems specified in the warning box are deleted from the entire language project.

    - Otherwise, click **Cancel**.
