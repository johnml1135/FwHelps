# About Complex Forms

*Using Tools › Lists tools*

In FLEx, *complex forms* are lexical entries that reference other entries as [components](../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Components_field.md) and reference an item from the **Complex Form Types** [list](List_item_usage_table.md).

[About entry types](About_Entry_Types.md) gives brief linguistic descriptions of the types. [Add a lexical subentry](../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Add_a_lexical_subentry.md) describes them complex form in **Lexicon**.

> [!TIP]
>
> - In the **Complex Form Types** list, you can [insert new types](Insert_a_list_item.md) or [subtypes](Insert_a_list_subitem.md).
>
> - In **Lexicon Edit**, you can [create](../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Create_a_lexical_entry.md) an entry that is a complex form or [change](../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/change_the_entry_type.md) an existing entry to be a complex form.
>
> - When you [use](../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Using_the_Configure_Dictionary_dialog_box.md) the **Configure** **Dictionary** dialog box, you configure entries that are [complex forms](../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Complex_Forms.md) in various places.
>
> **See Also:** [Dictionary layouts](../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Dictionary_views.md) and [Main Entry and Minor Entry](../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_Dictionary/Main_Minor_entry.md).
>
> **Reversal Indexes** is similar. **Classified Dictionary** is less similar because the list of fields is less hierarchical.
