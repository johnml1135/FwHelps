# Choose a Word Gloss Writing System

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Word Analyses*

In **Words Analyses**, you can choose which analysis writing system to display in the **Word Gloss** field.

1.  On the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then click **Word Analyses**.

2.  In the **Wordforms** pane, click the wordform that has the analysis that you will change.

3.  If necessary, [add a word gloss](Add_Word_Gloss.md).

4.  In the **Wordform Analyses** pane, click the **Word Gloss** field.

    A menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appears.

5.  Click the menu button, point to **Writing Systems**, and then click one of the following:

    - **Show all right now**

      All analysis writing systems are displayed while the focus remains in that field. When you click any other field, then writing systems without a check mark in this menu and without content in this field become hidden again.

    - One of the writing systems listed

      If the writing system you click has a check mark (![](../../../assets/images/CheckMark%20in%20RightClick%20Menu.png); is currently displayed), then that check mark is cleared. If that writing system had no content in the field, then it is removed from the **Word Gloss** field. If that writing system had content in the field, it remains displayed.

      Later, if a writing system does not have a check mark but there is content in the field in that writing system, then that writing system line will become hidden after that content is removed.

      If the writing system does not have a check mark (is *not* currently displayed), then a check mark is added and that writing system is displayed in the **Word Gloss** field.

    - **Configure**

      The **Writing Systems for Word Gloss field** opens. In the dialog box, select the writing systems you want to display with a check mark, and clear the check mark from each the writing system you do not want to display. Click **OK**.

    In the **Word Gloss** field, writing systems with a check mark are displayed, and writing systems without a check mark become hidden (*if unused*).
