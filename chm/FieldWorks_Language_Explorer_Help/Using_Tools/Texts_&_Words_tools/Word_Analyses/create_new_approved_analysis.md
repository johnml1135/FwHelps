# Add approved analysis

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Word Analyses*

For the current wordform, you can add a new analysis that will appear in the **User Approved (Analyses)** area.

1.  On the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then click **Word Analyses**.

2.  In the **Wordforms** pane, click the wordform for which you want to add an analysis.

3.  Do one of the following:

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Insert_toolbar.md) toolbar, click![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/CreateNewAnalysis.GIF).

    - On the **Insert** menu, click **Add Approved Analysis**.

    - Click the [User Approved (Analyses) field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_&_Words/user_approved_field.md), and then click the **Add Approved Analysis** link that appears.

      The **Insert Morpheme Breaks** dialog box appears.

4.  In the **Word** box, separate the morphemes with spaces and break characters. [Insert or remove morpheme breaks](../Interlinear_Texts/Insert_or_remove_morpheme_breaks.md) has important information to help you do this.

5.  Click **OK**.

    The new analysis appears under **User Approved (Analyses)**.

6.  To complete the analysis, do the following:

    - [Select the lexical sense](../Interlinear_Texts/Select_the_lexical_sense_for_a_morpheme.md) for each morpheme.

    - [Select a word category](../Interlinear_Texts/specify_a_word_category.md) in the [Word Category field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/word_category_field.md).

    - [Add a word gloss](Add_Word_Gloss.md).
