# Delete a wordform

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Word Analyses*

1.  On the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then click **Word Analyses** or **Word List Concordance**.

2.  Do *one* of the following:

    - In the **Wordforms** pane, click the wordform you want to delete. Then, right-click that wordform and select **Delete selected** **Wordform** in the menu that appears.

    - In the **Wordform Analyses** pane, click the area below the wordform, such as the space after the **Spelling Status** label. (Menu buttons ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) appear, one left of the wordform and another left of the **Spelling Status** label.) Click the menu button that is left of the *wordform*, and then select **Delete Wordform**

    The **Delete Wordform** warning box appears, and identifies the wordform. If the wordform occurs in any text, the warning box states that fact.

3.  If the wordform is used in at least one text, the **Delete** button is *not* available. In this case, click **Cancel** to close the warning box. [Delete the unwanted word](../Interlinear_Texts/Delete_unwanted_words.md) from **Texts & Words**, and then do the steps in this topic again.

    - The occurrence of the wordform may be in a text that is not currently included. In this case, include the text again to see the wordform in the text.

4.  If the wordform is *not* used in any text (**Number in Corpus** of **0** (zero)) and you are certain that you want to delete the wordform specified in the warning box, click **Delete**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - A **Number in Corpus** of **0** (zero) occurs for words used only in vernacular metadata (not in any text) and were added to the [word list](../Word_list_overview.md) while [spell checking vernacular words](../../../Basic_Tasks/Spell_Checking/vernacular_spell_checking.md). They remain in the vernacular spelling dictionary even if you delete them from the word list.
