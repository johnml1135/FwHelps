# Open Change Spelling dialog box

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Word Analyses › Change Spelling*

You can open this dialog box from nearly any tool or view where you can select the word or put the insertion point (cursor) in it. In **Word Analyses**, you can see the [word list](../../Word_list_overview.md), the [Spelling Status](../../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/spelling_status_field.md) field, and the analyses of the word. These may help you select occurrences most accurately.

To open the **Change Spelling** dialog box and display occurrences (in texts) of a word, do either of the following:

- In [Word Analyses](../Word_Analyses_overview.md), click the word in the **Wordforms** pane, and then do any of the following:

  - Click the [Spelling Status](../../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/spelling_status_field.md) field label, and then on the menu button ![](../../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF), click **Change Spelling**.

  - Right-click the word near the top of the **Wordform Analyses** pane (just above the **Spelling Status** field), and then click **Change Spelling**.

  - On the **Tools** menu, point to **Spelling**, and then click **Change Spelling**.

- In any [tool](../../../Using_Tools_overview.md) or view, select the desired word (or just click it). On the [Tools](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Tools_overview.md) menu, point to **Spelling**, and then click **Change Spelling**.

> [!TIP]
>
> - If you open the dialog box on a lexical entry that is *not* used in at least one analysis of a word in a text, there will be no occurrences. In this case, you cannot use the dialog box to change the spelling.
