# Specify spelling status

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Word Analyses*

You can specify that the spelling of the wordform (word) is *correct* or *incorrect*, or that you are *undecided*. Only words with spelling status set to **Correct** are included in the [vernacular spelling dictionary](../../../Basic_Tasks/Spell_Checking/dictionary_files.md).

1.  If the word is not already displayed in the **Wordform Analyses** pane, do *one* of the following:

    - Right-click an occurrence of the word, and then click **Show in Word Analyses**.

    - In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts** **&** **Words**, and then click **Word Analyses**. Then, click the word in the **Wordforms** pane.

    - On the [Tools](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Tools_overview.md) menu, point to **Spelling**, and then click **View Incorrect Words in Use**. Then, click the word in the **Wordforms** pane.

2.  In the [Spelling Status field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/spelling_status_field.md), select **Correct**, **Incorrect** or **Undecided**.

> [!TIP]
>
> - The [context-sensitive menu](../../../Basic_Tasks/Show_data/Context_sens_menus.md) command **Show in Word Analyses** is available in *many* places. Examples include any **Baseline** tab or [word focus box](../Interlinear_Texts/Word_Focus_Box_examples.md), and columnar views such as **Bulk Edit Wordforms**.\
>   From **Lexicon**, use [Show \<item\> in Concordance](../../../Basic_Tasks/Show_data/Show_in_from_Lexicon.md) to initially show it in a word focus box in the **Concordance**.
