# Delete word gloss

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Word Analyses*

1.  On the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then click **Word** **Analyses**.

2.  In the **Wordforms** pane, click the wordform that has the word gloss that you want to delete.

3.  In the **Wordform Analyses** pane, click the **Word Glosses** label for the word gloss you want to delete.

    A menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Word_Analyses/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appears.

4.  Do *one* of the following

    - Click the menu button that is to the left of the **Word Gloss** label, and then select **Delete Gloss**.

    - Right-click the **Word Gloss** label for the gloss you want to delete, and then select **Delete Gloss** on the menu that appears.

    The **Delete Word Gloss** warning box appears.

5.  If you are sure you want to delete the word gloss specified in the warning box, click **Delete**.
