# Click copy forms

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Bulk Edit Wordforms*

In [Bulk Edit Wordforms](Bulk_Edit_Wordforms_overview.md), the **Click Copy** tab allows you to copy words from one column to another with the simple click of your mouse. Currently, this is limited to the **Form** field content. However, you could display the **Form** field in the orthographic writing system and also in a phonetic or phonemic writing system, and copy between them.

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then click **Bulk Edit Wordforms**.

2.  Click the **Click Copy** tab.

3.  In the **Wordforms** pane, do one or more of the following to display the wordforms you will change.

    - Use the [Configure Columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Using_Configure_Columns_dialog_box.md) dialog box to hide/show/move columns, and specify the writing systems displayed, if necessary.

    - [Filter](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filtering_data_overview.md) or [sort](../../../Basic_Tasks/Sorting_data/Sorting_data_overview.md) the data to display only applicable wordforms.

4.  In the **Bulk Edit Operation** pane, do the following:

    - In the **Target Field** box, select the field into which you want to paste what you will click.

    - In the **Copy** area, to copy *one* word, select **Word**; to copy the *entire string* (phrase) of content, select **String, reordered at word clicked**.

    - In the **If** **target** **is not em****pty** area, select **Overwrite**, *or* **Append** and enter punctuation (semicolon, comma, or similar) in the **Separated by** box as a separator, if necessary.

5.  In a column, except the field specified in the **Target Field** box, click the word or string you want to copy.

    The word or string is copied into the column selected in the **Target Field** box.

    The changes are saved as you click.

> [!TIP]
>
> - The *reorder* feature automatically reorders words after the word you click.
>
>   For example, to copy "`to be happy`," you can click "`happy`" to yield "`happy, to be`" *or* click "`be`" to yield "`be happy, to`." If you click "`to`" the phrase is copied without any reordering.
>
> - If you selected **Append**, you can click the source phrase as many times as necessary to get the desired word order in the target field.
