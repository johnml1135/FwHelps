# Link words in a phrase

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts*

In a **Gloss** or **Analyze** tab, you can link words that make up a phrase. If you will use the [Text Chart](Text_Chart_tab/Text_chart_overview.md) tab, you need to honor separate words, keeping them separate. That is, be careful to only link words that are truly part of a phrase, such as an idiomatic phrase, and try to leave the words in verb phrases, noun phrases, locative phrases, and so on, separate.

Link words in a phrase as follows:

1.  Click the *first* word that makes up the phrase.

    The word appears in a [word focus box](Word_Focus_Box_examples.md). A **Link Word** button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Link_phrase_button.GIF) appears at the top corner, adjacent to the next word, unless the word is followed by a period, or other similar characters across which a phrase cannot exist.

2.  Do one of the following:

<!-- -->

1.  - Click the **Link Word** button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Link_phrase_button.GIF).

    - On the [Data](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Data/Data_overview.md) menu, click `Make phrase with next word`.

      The adjacent word moves into the current word focus box, with the words separated by a space.

<!-- -->

3.  For a phrase with more than two words, repeat the previous step until the entire phrase appears in one word focus box.

    When the entire phrase appears in a single word focus box, you can then [insert morpheme breaks](Insert_or_remove_morpheme_breaks.md) in the phrase. Then, [move to another word](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Data/Data_overview.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - The topic [Analyzing a lexical phrase](Analyzing_a_phrase.md) discusses phrases that have derivational or inflectional affixes.

> [!TIP]
>
> - You can use [Undo/Redo](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Edit/Undo_and_Redo.md) to change the last *link-words* operation, click the [break a phrase](Break_a_phrase.md) button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Break_phrase_button.GIF) on the toolbar, click **Break phrase into words** on the **Data** menu or press the shortcut keys `Ctrl+W` if you inadvertently include a word that is not part of the phrase.
>
> - If you link previously analyzed words as a phase, those existing analyses will be either
>
>   - removed from those words after they appear in the same word focus box, or
>
>   - replaced by a previous analysis of the same phrase, which you can either [approve or discard](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Data/Data_overview.md).
>
>   - If you link or break a phrase into words after they were inserted into a [text chart](Text_Chart_tab/Text_chart_overview.md), those words or phrases are removed from the text chart. You will need to [reinsert](Text_Chart_tab/reinsert_words_into_chart.md) them.
>
>   - If you [create a new lexical entry](Create_new_lex_entry_interlinear_view.md) with a complex form in the word focus box, the **Choose Lexical Entry or Sense** dialog box appears so you can choose the first entry or sense that is a [component](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Choose_components_for_Components_field.md) of the complex form.
