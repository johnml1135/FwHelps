> [!CAUTION]
>
> - Review the information in [Baseline text writing systems](baseline_text_writing_systems.md) *before* you do any interlinearizing of texts. It is possible to inadvertently create duplications in the [word list](../Word_list_overview.md) or lexical entries without proper headwords. This can take a lot of time to correct.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - The writing system of the *first word* in a text determines the directionality (left-to-right *or* right-to-left) for that entire text.
>
> - The only difference between the **Gloss** and **Analyze** tabs are the *default* lines that appear in the [word focus box](Word_Focus_Box_examples.md). Otherwise, they are simply two views of the same content, which you can [configure](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_interlinear_lines_dialog_box.md) separately.
>
> - - The task you do in the **Gloss** tab is often referred to as "glossing."
>
>   - The **Analyze** tab was called **Interlinearize** in early versions.
>
> - In the various panes, such as the **Texts** and **Concordance Results** panes, you can [configure the columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Using_Configure_Columns_dialog_box.md) to show any of the metadata about the texts.
>
> - [Swap Lexeme Form with Allomorph](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Swap_LexForm_with_Allomorph.md) allows you to exchange the forms in **Lexicon Edit** such that the change is also shown in analyses that use the forms.
>
> - By definition, a *baseline text* is the line of text that is the basis for all analysis, annotation, and translation. It is normally displayed as the top line in each segment.
