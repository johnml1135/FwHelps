# Select a word category

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts*

While you [gloss](specify_the_word_gloss.md) or manually [analyze](Analyze_Text_overview.md) wordforms (words) in an [interlinear view](Display_text_in_an_interlinear_view.md), you specify the category of each word in the **Word Cat** line. You can also specify a word category in [Word Analyses](../Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md), but only for analyses under **User Approved (Analyses)**. The changes you make in either area change both areas.

## Interlinear text

1.  [Display the text in an interlinear view](Display_text_in_an_interlinear_view.md).

2.  Click the word for which you will specify grammatical information.

    The word appears in a [focus box](Word_Focus_Box_examples.md).

3.  In the **Word Cat** line, click the down arrow ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Down_Arrow_pic.PNG), and then select one of the following:

    - a category from the list

    - **\<Not Sure\>**

    - **More** to open the [Add From Catalog](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Using_Add_from_Catalog_dialog_box.md) dialog box.

      If the category you need is not in the catalog, click the link so you can [create a custom category](../../Grammar_tools/Category_Edit/Insert_a_Category.md).

4.  When you are finished, [move to another word](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Data/Data_overview.md).

## Word Analyses

1.  On the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then click **Word** **Analyses**.

2.  In the **Wordforms** pane, click the desired word.

3.  In the **Wordform Analyses** pane, for the applicable analysis, click the down arrow ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Field_Types/down_arrow_button.png) in the [Word Category field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Texts_%26_Words/word_category_field.md), and then select one of the following:

    - a category from the list

    - **\<Not Sure\>**

    - **More** to open the [Add From Catalog](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Using_Add_from_Catalog_dialog_box.md) dialog box.
