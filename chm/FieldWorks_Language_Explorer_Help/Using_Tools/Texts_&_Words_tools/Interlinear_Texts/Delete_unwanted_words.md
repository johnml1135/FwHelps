# Delete unwanted words

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts*

To delete a word from the current text but *not* *from* the [word list](../Word_list_overview.md), do the following:

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Texts & Words**, and then select **Interlinear Texts**.

2.  In the **Texts** pane, click the text that has the word you want to delete.

3.  Click the **Baseline** tab.

4.  Select the word you want to delete, and then do one of the following:

    - On the **Edit** menu, click **Cut**.

    - Press the `Delete` key or the `Backspace` key.

    (*Do not click* ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Toolbars/RedDeleteRecordX.GIF) on the [Standard](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Standard_toolbar.md) toolbar. That will delete the *entire* *text*.)

The selected word is removed from the current text record. **Cut** (or `Ctrl+X`) saves the word to the clipboard so you can paste it back in the text. You can also use [Undo](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Edit/Undo_and_Redo.md) on the **Edit** menu to restore the word.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - *D*eleting a word in the **Baseline** tab does *not* remove the word from the word list. However, it can negatively affect the [Text Chart](Text_Chart_tab/Text_chart_overview.md) tab contents for that text. You can also use the [Change Spelling](../Word_Analyses/Change_Spelling/change_spelling_overview.md) feature to correct misspelled words.
>
> - To delete a word from the word list, see [Delete a wordform](../Word_Analyses/Delete_a_wordform.md) or [Bulk Edit Wordforms](../Bulk_Edit_Wordforms/Bulk_Edit_Wordforms_overview.md).
