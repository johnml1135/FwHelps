# About Add Words to Lexicon

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts*

**Add Words to Lexicon** is intended for use with *monomorphemic* languages or those with minimal morphology.

You can select this feature on the [Tools](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Tools_overview.md) menu or [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md).

The following functionality is enabled while this feature is selected:

- The labels of the **Word Gloss** and **Word Cat** lines are **Lex/Word Gloss** and **Lex/Word Cat** in the **Gloss** tab [word focus box](Word_Focus_Box_examples.md).

- The first time you approve unique contents in a word focus box (click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/GreenCheckMark.GIF)or equivalent), a new lexical entry is created:

  - The **Word** line content appears in the [Lexeme Form field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Entry_level_fields/Lexeme_Form_field.md).

  - The **Lex/Word Gloss** line content appears in the [Gloss field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Gloss_field_Sense.md).

  - The **Lex/Word Cat** line selection appears in the [Grammatical Info field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lexicon/Lexicon_Edit_fields/Sense_level_fields/Grammatical_Info_field.md).

- A new sense is added to the lexical entry if you select **Add New Sense** (**Lex/Word Gloss** line) *or* select a different category (**Lex/Word Cat** line).

> [!NOTE]
>
> - **Add Words to Lexicon** is on the [Information bar](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/information_bar_overview.md) *only* in **Interlinear Texts** (**Gloss** tab), but it is on the **Tools** menu when *any* **Gloss** tab is selected.
>
> - Other operations, such as **Add Word Gloss** or **Merge Gloss** in [Word Analyses](../Word_Analyses/Word_Analyses_overview.md), do *not* affect lexical entries even when **Add Words to Lexicon** is selected.
