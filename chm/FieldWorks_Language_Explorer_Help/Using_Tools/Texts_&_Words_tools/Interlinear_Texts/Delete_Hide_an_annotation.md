# Delete or Hide an annotation

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts*

You can delete or hide annotations (**Free**, **Lit**, or **Note** lines) for individual lines (segments) of text *independently*. Hiding lines preserves your annotation. It can help you use your available screen space more efficiently.

1.  [Display the text in an interlinear view](Display_text_in_an_interlinear_view.md).

2.  To *delete* words but keep the line, do the following:

    - Select the words you want to delete from the **Free**, **Lit**, or **Note** line.

    - Press the `Backspace` key or `Delete` key, or use the [shortcut keys](../../../User_Interface/Shortcuts/Shortcut_keys_to_edit_text.md) **Ctrl+X**.

3.  To *hide* a line, do one of these steps:

    - Right-click the line label (**Free**, **Lit.**, or **Note**), and then click the **Hide Free Translation**, **Hide Literal Translation**, or **Hide Note** command.

    - Click the line label (**Free**, **Lit.**, or **Note**), click the menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) and then click the **Hide Free Translation**, **Hide Literal Translation**, or **Hide Note** command.

4.  To *show* a line that was hidden, use the **Configure Interlinear Lines** dialog box.

5.  To *delete* a **Note** line and any note content, do the following:

    - Right-click any word in the note, or if the line is unused, right-click the white space where the note would be, and then click **Delete Note**.

That **Note** line and any content it contained are deleted.
