# Insert words into Text Chart

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

*Before* you insert any words or phrases into the chart, review the information under ![](../../../../assets/images/Important_Icon.gif) **Important** in the [Text Chart tab overview](Text_chart_overview.md) topic.

1.  In a **Text Chart** tab, vernacular words and their word glosses appear in the *bottom* pane of this tab. The first displayed word or phrase is selected. If appropriate, select one or more *subsequent* words that you want to insert into the chart into the same row (clause) and column.

2.  Do *one* of the following using the buttons at the bottom of the particular column into which you want to insert the word or words:

    - Click the button with the up-arrow (![](../../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Text_Chart_tab/Up_Arrow_chart.PNG)).\
      This inserts the selected word or words into that column in the *lowest* row.

    - Click the button with the down-arrow (![](../../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Text_Chart_tab/Dn_Arrow_chart.PNG)), and then select **Insert into new Row**.\
      This inserts the words into that column but in *new* row, which is a new *clause*.

    - Click the button with the down-arrow (![](../../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Text_Chart_tab/Dn_Arrow_chart.PNG)), point to **Insert as Moved from**, and then select a column.\
      This inserts the word or words into the chart in an order *other than* their default word order. The word(s) appear in the column you selected, and a [preposed from](Mark_as_preposed_from.md) or [postposed from](Mark_as_postposed_from.md) marker appears in the default column.

The selected word(s) are moved from the bottom pane into the chart column. The *next* word or phrase is automatically selected in the bottom pane so you can repeat the steps above until the bottom pane is empty.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - If you edit words or phrases after you inserted them into the chart, you need to [reinsert](reinsert_words_into_chart.md) them again.
>
> - You can [move cell contents](move_cell.md) or [move a word](move_word.md) between columns and [insert new rows](insert_row_below.md).
