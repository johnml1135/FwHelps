# Clear Chart from here on

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

When you use the `Text Chart` tab, you insert [glossed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart.

If you choose to change a significant amount of chart content, you can clear the chart from any cell (row and column) forward. If you need to [customize the columns](../../../Lists_tools/Text_Chart/specify_columns_for_chart.md), you may need to entirely delete all text chart contents. The words appear again in the lower pane so you can [insert](insert_words_into_chart.md) them back into the chart.

## Clear a portion of a chart

- Click the cell from which point you want to clear the chart, and then select **Clear chart from here on** in the menu that appears.

  The chart is cleared from that cell forward, including that cell.

## Delete an entire chart

- Click the cell that is in the first column and first row, and then select **Clear chart from here on** in the menu that appears.

  The entire text chart is cleared, which essentially deletes the chart.

> [!TIP]
>
> - You *can* work with metadata stored in [Text Chart Template fields](../../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Lists/Text_Chart_Temp_fields/Text_Chart_Temp_flds_overview.md) while you have data in your charts.
