# Make a Dependent Clause out of

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

In the **Text Chart** tab, you insert [glossed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart. Then, you can mark dependent clauses.

Two [colored](Text_Chart_tab_colors_lines.md) clause markers are used:

- Blue square brackets \[ \] appear around the dependent clause.

- Blue square brackets also appear in the initial cell (the cell you click to mark the clause). This pair of brackets contains one or more numbers and possibly also letters, such as \[2a-2b\], and serve as a *place holder* for the dependent clause.

## Make a Dependent Clause out of

1.  In a **Text Chart** tab, if the dependent clause *precedes* the independent clause, click the cell that precedes the independent clause (usually the preceding **Outer** column). *Or*, if the dependent clause *follows* the independent clause, click the cell that follows the independent clause (usually the following **Outer** column).

2.  Point to **Make a Dependent Clause out of** on the menu that appears, and then click one of the following:

    If the **Select Clauses** dialog box appears, select the first *previous* row or last *following* row of the dependent clause. Then, *all the rows* that comprise the dependent clause, up to the independent clause, are marked as dependent. Click **OK**.

    Clause markers appear.

- **Previous Clause**

- One of the available **Next Clause** commands

- **Other**

## Remove Clause Markers

- In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell that has the *place-holder* marker, and then click **Remove Clause Marker** on the menu that appears.\
  The two clause markers and associated colors are removed from the chart.
