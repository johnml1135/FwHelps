# Reinsert words into Text Chart

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

When you use a **Text Chart** tab, you [insert](insert_words_into_chart.md) glossed or analyzed vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart. Initially, vernacular words and their word glosses appear in the bottom pane. The first displayed word or phrase is selected.

If you type a new word into the **Baseline** tab after you have inserted the words into the chart, you may see one of these results:

- If you typed the word between two word that are in the *same* cell, the new word also appears in that cell.

- If you typed a word *after* a word that is in one cell, but *before* a word that is in a different cell, then the new word appears in the bottom pane. Cells that can contain the inserted word have a green background color, and only those columns have an available up arrow (![](../../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Text_Chart_tab/Up_Arrow_chart.PNG)) button.

  - Click the up arrow button for the appropriate column.

The word is inserted into the text chart. If necessary, use other commands, such as [Move Word](move_word.md) or [Move Cell](move_cell.md) to finish marking the chart.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - Language Explorer tries to preserve the word order of the **Baseline** tab content in the **Text Chart** tab, but there are limits.
>
> - - If you make numerous complex edits or also add new content in the middle of a text, the complexity of the reinserting it all back into the text chart may result in words getting out of order. In this case, you will need to [clear the chart](clear_chart_from.md) from the point where the words are not in the correct order.
