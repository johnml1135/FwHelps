# Make a Speech Clause out of

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

When you use a **Text Chart** tab, you insert [glossed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart. Then, as needed, you can mark quoted text as a speech clause. For a conversation, see **Note** below.

Two [colored](Text_Chart_tab_colors_lines.md) clause markers are used:

- <a href="" class="popupspot" style="color: #008000;">Green</a> square brackets \[ \] appear around the word or words in the speech clause, which also become green in color and receive a dashed underline.

- Green square brackets also appear in the initial cell (the cell you clicked). These contain a number and possibly also a letter (such as \[2a-2d\]). These indicate the rows that hold the speech clause.

## Make a Speech Clause out of

- In a **Text Chart** tab, click that cell that appears immediately before or after a speech clause. Point to **Make a Speech Clause out of** on the menu that appears, and then click one of the following:

  - **Previous Clause**

  - One of the available **Next Clause** commands

  - **Other**

  If the **Select Clauses** dialog box appears, select the first *previous* clause or last *subsequent* clause that is part of the speech clause. This selection specifies that the selected clause (row) and *all the clauses* up to the current cell are part of the same speech clause. Click **OK**.

  Clause markers appear.

## Remove Clause Markers

- In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell that has the speech clause markers (such as \[2a-2d\]) you want to remove, and then click **Remove Clause Marker** on the menu that appears.

  The associated speech clause markers and colors are removed from the chart.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - Currently, the chart does not allow you to mark individual speakers in a conversation text. In the **Notes** column, you could enter the name or initials of the speaker, or a speaker letter or number. Optionally, [export](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Export/Export_Discourse_Chart.md) the chart to Microsoft Word and then manually apply different colors to a portion of the speech clauses. For example, the square brackets \[ \] around speech clauses could be a different color for each speaker.
