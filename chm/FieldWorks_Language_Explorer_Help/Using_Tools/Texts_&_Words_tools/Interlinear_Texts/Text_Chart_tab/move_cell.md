# Move Cell

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

When you use the **Text Chart** tab, you insert [glossed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart. You can move all the words in a cell forward or back to a different column.

- In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell that has the words you want to move. Point to **Move Cell** on the menu that appears, and then select **Forward** or **Back**.

  All of the words in the cell move in the selected direction, according to the direction of the vernacular writing system whether left-to-right *or* right-to-left.

  Markers (**Preposed from**, **Postposed from**, **Grammatical Information**, and so on) do *not* move, so you need to review your markers. Remove and replace them as necessary.

> [!TIP]
>
> - If you need to move the words more than one column, do the steps above for the first move, and the you can use the [shortcut keys](../../../../User_Interface/Shortcuts/shortcut_keys_Texts_Words_tools.md) `Ctrl+Forward Arrow` or `Ctrl+Back Arrow` to repeat the last command (forward or back, respectively).
>
> - - Language Explorer preserves the word order, so you may move more words with each subsequent move command.
>
> - You can [insert a new row below](insert_row_below.md) the current row and move cell content down to that row.
>
> - You can also [move a single word](move_word.md) from a cell either forward or backward.
