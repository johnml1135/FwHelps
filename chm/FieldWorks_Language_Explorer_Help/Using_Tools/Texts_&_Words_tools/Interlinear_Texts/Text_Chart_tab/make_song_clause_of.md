# Make a Song Clause out of

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

When you use the **Text Chart** tab, you insert [glossed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart. Then, as needed, you can mark a song clause.

Two [colored](Text_Chart_tab_colors_lines.md) clause markers are used:

- Purple square brackets \[ \] appear around the word or words in the song clause.

- Purple square brackets also appear in the initial cell (the cell you clicked). These contain a number and possibly also a letter (such as \[2a-2d\]). These indicate the rows that hold the song clause.

## Make a Song Clause out of

- In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell that appears immediately before or after a song clause. Point to **Make a Song** **Clause out of** on the menu that appears, and then click one of the following:

  - **Previous Clause**

  - One of the available **Next Clause** commands

  - **Other**

If the **Select Clauses** dialog box appears, select the first *previous* clause or last *subsequent* clause that is part of the song clause. This selection specifies that the selected clause (row) and *all the clauses* up to the current cell are part of the same song clause. Click **OK**.

Clause markers appear.

## Remove Clause Markers

- In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell that has a song clause marker (such as \[2a-2d\]) you want to remove, and then click **Remove Clause Marker** on the menu that appears.

The associated song clause markers and colors are removed from the chart.
