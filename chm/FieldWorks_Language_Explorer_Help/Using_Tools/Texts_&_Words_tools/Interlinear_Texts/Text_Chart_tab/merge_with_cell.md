# Merge with Cell

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

When you use the **Text Chart** tab, you insert [glossed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart. Then, as needed, you can a merge a cell with *empty* cells *before* or *after* it.

This does not actually *merge* content or cells, as the menu commands continue to reflect the original nature of *each* cell. However, it allows cell contents to "overlap" into adjacent empty cells. To goal is to help you use the available screen space and paper (if [printed](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Print.md)) as efficiently as possible. The merged cells also appear in [exported](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Export/Export_Discourse_Chart.md) charts.

## Merge cells

In a **Text Chart** tab, do the following to merge adjacent cells:

- Click the cell you want to merge with an adjacent empty cell or cells, and then click **Merge with Cell before this** or **Merge with Cell after this** on the menu that appears.

(There must be an empty cell next to the cell you clicked, for the menu commands to appear.)

The cell with content and the adjacent cell or cells appear to be one cell and the content uses all of the area of those cells. Also, a check mark appears next to the menu command you clicked.

## Remove cell merging

In a **Text Chart** tab, do the following to reverse a previous merge:

- Click the original cell (*not* a merged-with cell), and then click the **Merge with Cell before this** or **Merge with Cell after this** menu command again.

This removes the check mark and returns the cells to the original not-merged state.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - Some other menu commands may cause an automatic removal of a previous merge. This can happen, for example, when a previously-empty cell receives an inserted word or other content.
