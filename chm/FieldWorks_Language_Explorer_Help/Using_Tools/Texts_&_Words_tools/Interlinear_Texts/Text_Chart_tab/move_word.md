# Move Word

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

When you use the **Text Chart** tab, you insert [glossed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyzed](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) vernacular words and their word glosses into the chart. You can move a word forward and back to a different column.

To preserve word order, Language Explorer automatically limits the move operation to words that can only move forward and those that can only move back.

To move a word forward or back, do the following:

- In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell that has word you want to move. Point to **Move Word** on the menu that appears, and then select **Forward** or **Back**.

  The word that is permitted to move forward or back moves in the direction you selected.

  Markers (**Preposed from**, **Postposed from**, **Grammatical Information**, and so on) do *not* move, so you need to review your markers. Remove and replace them as necessary.

> [!TIP]
>
> - If you need to move a word more than one column, do the steps above for the first move, and the you can use the [shortcut keys](../../../../User_Interface/Shortcuts/shortcut_keys_Texts_Words_tools.md) `Ctrl+Forward Arrow` or `Ctrl+Back Arrow` to repeat the last command (forward or back, respectively).
>
> - - Language Explorer preserves the word order, so you may move more words with each subsequent move command.
>
> - You can [insert a new row below](insert_row_below.md) the current row and move a word down to that row.
>
> - You can [move all the words](move_cell.md) in a cell.
