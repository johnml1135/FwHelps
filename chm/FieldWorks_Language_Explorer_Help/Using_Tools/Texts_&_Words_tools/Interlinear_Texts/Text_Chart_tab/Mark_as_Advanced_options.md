# Mark as Advanced options

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

You use the **Mark As Preposed - Advanced Options** or **Mark As Postposed - Advanced Options** dialog box to mark one or more *contiguous words* in a cell as preposed or postposed, *without marking all the words* in that cell. You can choose to mark the words as preposed or postposed from the current or different clause (row).

Two [colored](Text_Chart_tab_colors_lines.md) markers are used:

- Words marked as preposed or postposed become red in color.

- A pink \>\> marker appears in the cell (row and column) that is the default position of the word(s).

## Mark words

1.  In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell that has the word or words you want to mark.

2.  Do one of the following on the menu that appears:

    - Point to **Mark as** **Preposed from**, and then click **Advanced**.

      The **Mark as Preposed -- Advanced options** dialog box appears.

    - Point to **Mark as Postposed from**, and then click **Advanced**.

      The **Mark as Postposed -- Advanced options** dialog box appears.

3.  In the dialog box, do the following:

    - Select the row (clause) and column that indicate the default position (receives the \>\> marker).

    - Select the word or words you want to mark. (Click the first word and drag the mouse pointer across the next word or words. Alternatively, click the first word, and then press `Shift+Right Arrow` to select the next word.)

    - Click **OK**.

    The two colored markers appear in the chart. Also, a check mark is added to the submenu command that shows the column you selected.

## Remove markers

1.  In a **Text Chart** tab, click the cell with the word or words that are marked as preposed or postposed.

2.  Point to **Mark as Preposed from** or **Mark as Postposed from** on the menu that appears, and then select the column that has a check mark. (The column with a check mark must match the column with the associated pink \<\< marker.)

    The two colored markers are removed from the chart. The check mark is cleared from the submenu.
