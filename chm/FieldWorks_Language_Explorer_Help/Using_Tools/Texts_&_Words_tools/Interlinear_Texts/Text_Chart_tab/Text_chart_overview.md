# Text Chart tab overview

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts › Text Chart tab*

The **Text Chart** tab is available in [Interlinear Texts](../texts_edit_overview.md), [Concordance](../../Concordance/Concordance_overview.md), [Complex Concordance](../../Complex_Concordance/Complex_Concordance_overview.md) and [Word List Concordance](../../Word_List_Concordance/Word_List_Concordance_overview.md). It is based on the Stephen H. Levinsohn discourse analysis model.

It enables those without extensive morphemic knowledge of the source texts and without extensive computer skills, to quickly chart texts, without getting bogged down with formatting details. You can organize information needed by a linguistic consultant to analyze, for example, the discourse features of the language. You can [print](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Print.md) a charted text or to [export](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Export/Export_Discourse_Chart.md) a chart (XML formats). See also: [About Text Chart](About_Text_Chart_tab.md) tab.

The tasks that you typically need to do are listed below in an order that is recommended for most normal situations.

1.  *Before* you use the chart:

    - Use the **Gloss** tab to [gloss](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) the text *or* use the **Analyze** tab to [analyze](../Analyze_Text_overview.md) the text. See ![](../../../../assets/images/Important_Icon.gif) **Important** below.

    - In [Lists](../../../Lists_tools/Lists_overview.md), select the template you want to use for this text. Then, verify or [specify the columns](../../../Lists_tools/Text_Chart/specify_columns_for_chart.md) (their names, order and so on) needed for the charting goal for this text.\
      \
      You should *not* change them *after* they are specified and used in one or more text charts. This helps ensure the integrity of those charts.

    <!-- -->

    - [Add a new text constituent chart template](../../../Lists_tools/Text_Chart/Add_a_new_Text_Constituent_Chart_Template.md) if you want to chart this text with different discourse factors in focus.

    - Verify or edit the [Text Chart Markers](../../../Lists_tools/Text_Chart/Text_Chart_Markers.md) list of grammatical information.

2.  Use the chart:

    - Clink the **Text Chart** tab if it is not already displayed.

    - Click the down arrow (![](../../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/TextChartChooser.png)) that is near the *top* of the **Text Chart** tab. Click the template you will use, if the correct one is not already shown there.

    - [Insert words into the chart](insert_words_into_chart.md) (Includes **Insert into new Row**, **Insert as Moved from** and [Insert Row Below](insert_row_below.md)

    - [Merge with Cell before/after this](merge_with_cell.md)

    - Reposition text data:

      [Move Cell](move_cell.md) (all words)

      [Move Word](move_word.md) (one word)

    - [Mark Last Row](Mark_last_row.md) (of sentence or paragraph)

    - Mark clause types:

      [Make a Dependent Clause out of](make_dependent_clause_of.md)

      [Make a Speech Clause out of](make_speech_clause_of.md)

      [Make a Song Clause out of](make_song_clause_of.md)

    - Mark as preposed/postposed:

      [Mark as Preposed from](Mark_as_preposed_from.md)

      [Mark as Postposed from](Mark_as_postposed_from.md)

    [Advanced options](Mark_as_Advanced_options.md) **Mark as Preposed from** or **Mark as Postposed from**

    - [Mark Grammatical Information](mark_grammatical_information.md)

    - [Mark as Missing](mark_as_missing.md) (Implicit content)

    - [Clear Chart from here on](clear_chart_from.md) (delete chart content)

    - [Change Text Chart tab font attributes](change_text_chart_font.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - [Gloss](../specify_the_word_gloss.md) or [analyze](../Analyze_Text_overview.md) the text *before* you insert any words into the chart.
>
>   - Make sure that the **Baseline** tab content is correct, such as spelling of words. If you edit words in the **Baseline** tab after they are in a chart, those edited words are removed from the chart. In this case, you will need to [reinsert words](reinsert_words_into_chart.md) into the chart. Similar problems can occur if you enter additional words in the **Baseline** tab. *If you make too many edits, FieldWorks may be unable to preserve the word order. In this case, you will need to clear the chart from some* *point in the chart and then insert the words again.*
>
>   - Make sure each word has at least one word (gloss) in the **Word Gloss** line. Later, you can change the gloss of a word without negatively affecting the chart.
>
>   - Make sure to honor separate words (keep them unlinked); such as all clitics and separate words in verb phrases, noun phrases, locative phrases and so on (*except* idiomatic phrases, like 'kick the bucket' which you may [link](../Link_words_in_phrase.md)).
>
>   - You can later finish the interlinearization ([analyze](../Analyze_Text_overview.md)) to the morpheme level in the **Analyze** tab if you do not change any spellings and phrase links.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - **Notes** column: This is an editable text column in which you can type notation or comments about the clause in that row.
>
> You can drag and drop the **Notes** column to move it so it is either the first or last column.
>
> - You can access the **Text Chart** tab in **Interlinear Texts**, **Concordance** and **Word List Concordance**. The **Interlinear Texts** is recommend because there you can most easily maximize the size of the pane.
>
> - You will likely want to maximize the window, and then [resize](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/Window/resizing_window_panes.md) the [Navigation pane](../../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Navigation/Navigation_Pane_overview.md) and the center **Texts** pane until they are very narrow.
>
> - You can use [Undo](../../../../User_Interface/Menus/Edit/Undo_and_Redo.md) to remove the most recent changes, unless you have left the tab or refreshed the window in any way.
>
> - **See:** <a href="https://www.sil.org/~levinsohns/discourse.htm" target="_blank" title="https://www.sil.org/~levinsohns/discourse.htm">https://www.sil.org/~levinsohns/discourse.htm</a> and associated pages for more information about discourse.
>
> - For example, from this page (*Introduction to 'Discourse for Translation' Files*), click *narrative* to access the *Self-Instruction Materials on Narrative Discourse Analysis* course book (pdf).
