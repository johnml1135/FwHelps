# Break a phrase

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts*

To break a phrase of [linked words](Link_words_in_phrase.md) back into separate words, do the following in any **Gloss** tab or **Analyze** tab:

1.  Click the phrase.

    The phrase appears in a [word focus box](Word_Focus_Box_examples.md).

2.  Do one of the following:

    - Click the **Break Phrase** button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Interlinear_Texts/Break_phrase_button.GIF) on the bottom of the word focus box (or on the toolbar)

    - Press the [shortcut keys](../../../User_Interface/Shortcuts/shortcut_keys_Texts_Words_tools.md) `Ctrl+W`

    - On the [Data](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Data/Data_overview.md) menu, click `Break phrase into words`.

      All the words that made up the phrase become separate words which you can analyze separately.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - If you break a phrase, and occurrences of those individual words have previous analyses as separate words, the following will occur:
>
>   - The word that had been the first word in the phrase appears in the current word focus box, and it receives a previous analysis that you can either [approve or discard](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Data/Data_overview.md).
>
>   - Then, if you move the focus to the next word that had been in a phrase, it also receives a previous analysis that you can approve or discard.
