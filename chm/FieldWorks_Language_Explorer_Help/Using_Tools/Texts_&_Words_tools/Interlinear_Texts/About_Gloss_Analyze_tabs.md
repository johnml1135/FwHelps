# About Gloss and Analyze tabs

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Interlinear Texts*

In **Texts & Words**, the **Gloss** tab and **Analyze** tab are two interlinear views to **Baseline** tab contents. They are basically the *same* except for these differences:

- The **Gloss** tab includes the [Add Words to Lexicon](About_Add_Words_to_Lexicon.md) feature.

- The lines (**Word**, **Morphemes**, **Word Gloss**, and so on) that appear *by default* are different. The [Configure Interlinear lines](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Tools/Configure_interlinear_lines_dialog_box.md) dialog box, and various [right-click menu commands](Configure_Interlinear_lines_right_click.md) allow you to change which lines are displayed in a tab independently.

The following topics help you learn the uses for these tabs:

- [Texts & Words overview](../Texts_and_Words_overview.md) gives an important overview of what you do with *texts* and the *wordforms* (words) in them.

  - [Interlinear Texts overview](texts_edit_overview.md) lists all the tabs.

- [Parsing words overview](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Parser/Parsing_words_overview.md) gives an important overview for using computational parsers.

  - [Analyze text overview](Analyze_Text_overview.md) lists tasks that you typically do *manually*.

- [Specify a word gloss](specify_the_word_gloss.md) gives steps to only add word glosses. You can specify word glosses in either the **Gloss** or **Analyze** tab, unless you want to [add words to the lexicon](About_Add_Words_to_Lexicon.md). The **Gloss** tab is convenient if you will use the **Text Chart** tab before you fully analyze the text.
