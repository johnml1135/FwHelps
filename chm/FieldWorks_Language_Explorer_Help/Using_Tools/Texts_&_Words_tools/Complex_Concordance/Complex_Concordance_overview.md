# Complex Concordance overview

*Using Tools › Texts & Words tools › Complex Concordance*

In **Texts & Words**, the **Complex Concordance** tool allows you specify search criteria that can be more specific, compared to the **Concordance** tool.

You can search for items in a particular context. Examples (*not exhaustive*):

- nouns that are followed by a verb

- wordforms (words) that are followed by a particular tag

- morphemes of a particular grammatical category

- words or morphemes followed by a number.

## Specify Concordance Criteria

This is the *upper* portion of the center pane. A line separates the top portion from the **Insert options** links. If you click above the line, the *insertion point* appears as a blinking vertical line. This is where the search criteria appear as you specify them ([examples](Complex_Concordance_examples.md)).

To [specify](Specify_Complex_Concordance_criteria.md) complex concordance criteria, you can

- choose one or more elements of criteria for [morphemes](Set_Morph_Criteria.md), [words](Set_Word_Criteria.md), or [tags](Select_Tag.md)

- insert the Boolean logical disjunction **OR**

- insert word boundary **\#** markers

- [set occurrence values](Set_Occurrence_(Complex_Concordance).md).

The ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Complex_Concordance/SearchButton.png) (**Search**) button is at the right end of the line.

## **Concordance Results**

This is the *lower* portion of the center pane. You can [configure columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Configure_Columns_overview.md), and [sort](../../../Basic_Tasks/Sorting_data/Sort_data.md) and [filter](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filter_data.md) the results.

Results are based on the texts which are *not* [filtered](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filter_Texts_Words.md) out in anyway.
