# Insert a morpheme ad hoc rule

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Ad hoc Rules*

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Grammar**, and then click **Ad hoc Rules**.

2.  Do one of the following:

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Insert/Insert_overview.md) toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/Create_MorphemeCoProhibition_Grammar.GIF).

    - On the **Insert** menu, click **Rule to prevent morpheme co-occurrence**.

    A new ad hoc rule is inserted.

3.  In the **Rule to Prevent Morpheme Co-occurrence** pane, do the following:

    - Click the **Key Morpheme** field, and then click the ellipsis button ![](../../../assets/images/Ellipsis_button.PNG) that appears. In the **Choose** **Morpheme and Grammatical Info** dialog box, enter a form of a lexical entry in the **Find** box. In the **Lexical Entries** area, select the desired entry. In the **Grammatical Info** box, select the appropriate grammatical information, if necessary. Click **OK**.

    - Select an item for the **Cannot Occur** field.

    - Click the **Other Morpheme(s)** field, and then click the ellipsis button that appears. In the **Choose Morpheme and Grammatical Info** dialog box, enter a form of a lexical entry in the **Find** box. In the **Lexical Entries** area, select the desired entry. In the **Grammatical Info** box, select the appropriate grammatical information, if necessary. Click **OK**.

      **Note:** If two or more morphemes are selected, the relative order of them is crucial. They *must* appear in the linear order that they would appear in the word.

> [!TIP]
>
> - If you cannot find the desired lexical entry in the dialog box, you may need to select a different writing system in the **Writing System** box.
>
> - Once the [Key Morpheme](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Ad_hoc_Rules_fields/Key_Morpheme_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) field has content, you *cannot* select and delete that content. Instead, replace the content with other content.
>
> - For a more complete description of ad hoc rules, point to **Resources** on the [Help](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu, and then click **Introduction to Parsing**.
