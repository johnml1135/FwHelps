# Insert an ad hoc co-occurrence group

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Ad hoc Rules*

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Grammar**, and then click **Ad hoc Rules**.

2.  Do one of the following:

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Insert_toolbar.md) toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/Create_CoProhibitionGroup_Grammar.GIF).

    - On the **Insert** menu, click **Group of ad hoc rules**.

    A new group of ad hoc rules is inserted.

3.  In the **Group of Ad hoc Rules** pane, do the following:

    - Enter content in the [Name](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Ad_hoc_Rules_fields/Name_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) and [Description](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Ad_hoc_Rules_fields/Description_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) fields.

    - Click or right-click the [Members](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Ad_hoc_Rules_fields/Members_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) field label.

      Links and a menu button ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Ad_hoc_Rules/Menu_Button_pic.GIF) appear. The commands you can select are **Insert Morpheme Rule**, **Insert Allomorph Rule** and **Insert Nested Group**.

    - Click the applicable link or menu command to insert the desired rule or nested group. (For a nested group, see step **4** below.)

    - Choose contents for the **Key Morpheme**/**Allomorph**, **cannot occur**, and **Other Morpheme(s)**/**Allomorph(s)** fields. See ![](../../../assets/images/Important_Icon.gif) **Important** below.

    - Repeat the applicable step(s) again until the group is completed.

    4.  To create a *nested* group, click the **Members**, and then click the **Insert Nested Group** link.

    - Enter content in the [Nested Group Name](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Ad_hoc_Rules_fields/Nested_Group_Name_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) and [Nested Group Description](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Ad_hoc_Rules_fields/Description_field_Ad_hoc_Rules.md) fields for the nested group.

    - Click the [Nested Group Members](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Ad_hoc_Rules_fields/nested_group_members_field_ad_hoc_rules.md) field label, and then click one of these links that appear: **Insert Morpheme Rule**, **Insert Allomorph Rule** or **Insert Nested Group**.

    - Choose contents for the **Key Morpheme**/**Allomorph**, **cannot occur**, and **Other Morpheme(s)**/**Allomorph(s)** fields. See ![](../../../assets/images/Important_Icon.gif) **Important** below.

    - Repeat the applicable step(s) again until the nested group is completed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - If two or more morphemes or allomorphs are selected in a field, the relative order of them is crucial. They *must* appear in the linear order that they would appear in the word.
>
> - Refer to [Insert a morpheme ad hoc rule](Insert_a_morpheme_ad_hoc_rule.md) or [Insert an allomorph ad hoc rule](insert_an_allomorph_ad_hoc_rule.md), for additional information, if necessary.
>
> - The content comes from the [Lexicon](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_overview.md). For more details, see [Ad hoc Rules field content sources](Ad_hoc_Rules_field_content_sources.md).
