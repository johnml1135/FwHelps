# Insert a natural class

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Natural Classes*

You can insert a natural class that is defined by its phonemes *or* by phonological features.

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Grammar**, and then click **Natural Classes**.

2.  To insert a natural class defined by its phonemes, do one of the following:

    - On the **Insert** toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Natural_Classes/Create_NaturalClass_Grammar.GIF).

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Insert/Insert_overview.md) menu, click **Natural Class (phonemes)**.

    - Press the [shortcut keys](../../../User_Interface/Shortcuts/shortcut_keys_Grammar_tools.md) `Ctrl+I`.

    A natural class is added.

3.  To insert a natural class defined by phonological features, do one of the following:

    - On the **Insert** toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Toolbars/Add_Feature_Grammar.GIF).

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Insert/Insert_overview.md) menu, click **Natural Class (features)**.

    A natural class is added.

4.  Enter a name, description, and an abbreviation (the *abbreviation* , such as **V** or **C** is used to represent the natural classes in [environments](../Environments/Insert_an_environment.md)).

5.  Do one of the following, depending on which field appears in the pane:

    - [Choose phonemes](Choose_Phonemes_for_Natural_Class.md).

    - [Choose phonological features](Choose_phonological_features(NC).md).

> [!NOTE]
>
> - [Edit a natural class](Edit_a_natural_class.md) has important information.
