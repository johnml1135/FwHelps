# Insert a grapheme

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Phonemes*

In the **Phonemes** [Grammar tool](../grammar_overview.md), you can insert an *additional* [Grapheme field](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Phonemes_fields/representation_field_phonemes.md) for each of the ways a phoneme is written (can appear) in the orthography. To do this, do the following steps:

1.  In the **Navigation** **Pane**, click **Grammar**, and then click **Phonemes**.

2.  In the **Phonemes** pane, click the phoneme to which you will add a grapheme.

3.  In the **Phoneme** pane, click the **In Orthography as** field label.

    A link and a menu button (![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Phonemes/Menu_Button_pic.GIF)) appear.

4.  Do one of the following:

    - Click the **Insert Grapheme** link.

    - Click the menu button, and then click **Insert** **Grapheme**.

    - Right-click the **In Orthography as** field label, and the select **Insert** **Grapheme**.

    - On the **Insert** menu, click **Grapheme**.

    Another **Grapheme** field appears under the existing field(s).

5.  Click the **Grapheme** label, and then type the grapheme of the phoneme.

    - If more than one writing system is [displayed](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Field_Types/Single_line_text_field_example_graphic.md) in the **Grapheme** field, enter the applicable grapheme in each of those writing systems.
