# Categories Browse overview

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Categories Browse*

In the **Grammar** area, *categories* are shown in two tools: [Category Edit](../Category_Edit/Category_Edit_overview.md) and **Categories Browse**. This is because the list of categories is hierarchical. The other grammar area lists are *not* hierarchical, so their "browse tool" equivalent is the *center pane* in the respective [grammar tool](../grammar_overview.md).

**Categories Browse** is primarily *read-only* so you cannot edit the data there. However, you can easily show a category in **Category Edit** from **Categories Browse**, and the following:

- Click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Categories_Browse/Insert_Category.GIF) on the **Insert** toolbar to [insert a new category](../Category_Edit/Insert_a_Category.md).

- [Delete](delete_items_in_categories_browse.md) a category.

- [Configure the columns](../../../Basic_Tasks/Configure_Columns/Configure_Columns_overview.md) to customize the display.

- [Filter](../../../Basic_Tasks/Filtering_data/filter_grammar_data.md) and [sort](../../../Basic_Tasks/Sorting_data/Sort_Grammar_data.md) the displayed data.

- [Print](../../../User_Interface/Menus/File/Print.md) the contents of the view *as displayed*, including sorts, filters, and so on.

> [!NOTE]
>
> - The [sort](../../../Basic_Tasks/Sorting_data/Sort_Grammar_data.md) order in the **Categories Browse** controls the order in the center pane of [Category Edit](../Category_Edit/Category_Edit_overview.md).
