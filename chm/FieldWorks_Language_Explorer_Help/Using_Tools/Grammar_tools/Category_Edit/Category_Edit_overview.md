# Category Edit overview

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Category Edit*

In **Category Edit**, you manage grammatical categories (or *parts of speech*) that are referenced by vernacular lexical entries and used by computational [parsers](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Parser/Parsing_words_overview.md).

A *separate* list of grammatical categories for [reversal indexes](../../Lexicon_tools/Reversal_Indexes/reversal_indexes_overview.md) is stored in the [Reversal Index Categories](../../Lists_tools/Lists_overview.md) list, which is the **Lists** area.

### ![](../../../assets/images/Important_Icon.gif) Important - Category Hierarchy

In FieldWorks Language Explorer (FLEx), categories are organized hierarchically. The particular hierarchy in your language project affects how FieldWorks Language Explorer (FLEx) and the computational parsers handle the following:

- **Inflectional affix templates and their slots**

  When you define an [affix template](Insert_an_affix_template.md) for a category, that template will be tried by the parser for any stem of that category *or* a stem of any of its nested categories. Thus, you can capture generalizations about the inflectional templates by placing common inflectional templates *higher* in the hierarchy.

  Inflectional affix slots behave similarly with respect to the hierarchy. When you define the [slots](Insert_an_Affix_Slot.md) for a category, those slots may be used in any template for this category and any of its nested categories.

- **Inflection classes**

  When you define an [Inflection class](Insert_an_Inflection_Class.md) or subclass at a particular category in the hierarchy, then that class is available to be used for any lexical item associated with that category *or* any of its nested categories. Thus, you will probably want to define your inflection classes at the *highest* appropriate level in the hierarchy to capture generalizations.

- **Derivational affixes**, categories of

  You can use the hierarchy to capture some generalizations. For example, when you choose a category for the **Attaches to Category** box ([Create a lexical entry](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Create_a_lexical_entry.md), [Edit Grammatical Info](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Using_Edit_Gram_Infodlg.md), and so on), FLEx will allow the derivational affix to apply to stems of this category and any of its nested categories.

- **Compound Rules**

  When you indicate the information for a left or right member of a [compound rule](../Compound_Rules/Compound_Rules_overview.md), FLEx will consider stems of this category *and* any of its nested categories to match.

- **Stem Allomorph Labels**

  Subcategories will inherit any [Stem Allomorph Labels](Insert_a_Stem_Allomorph_Label.md) which their parent categories have, so you will probably want to create the Stem Allomorph Label the *highest* level category.

- For more information and examples, point to **Resources** on the [Help](../../../User_Interface/Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu, and then click **Introduction to Parsing**. See sections:

  - **Inflection and Categories Considerations**

  - **Inflection Classes**

  - **Category-changing Derivational Affixes and Category Organization**

  - **Compound Rules and Categories Considerations**

  - **Stem Allomorph Labels**, and

  - others.
