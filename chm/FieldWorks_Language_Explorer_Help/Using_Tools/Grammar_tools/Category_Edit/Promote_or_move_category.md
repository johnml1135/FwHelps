# Promote or move category

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Category Edit*

In **Category Edit**, you can promote a subcategory, or move a category or subcategory. You cannot use Undo (![](../../../assets/images/User_Interface/Toolbars/Undo.gif)) to reverse a promotion or move. Instead, just move the item.

## Promote

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Grammar**, and then click **Category Edit**.

2.  In the center pane, right-click the subcategory, and the select **Promote**.

    The subcategory moves up one level in the hierarchy.

## Move

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Grammar**, and then click **Category Edit**.

2.  In the center pane, click the category or subcategory you want to move, hold the mouse button down, drag the pointer to a different place in the hierarchy, and then release the mouse button.

    The category or subcategory moves to the place where you released the button.

> [!TIP]
>
> - Normally, with the [Add from catalog](../../Lexicon_tools/Lexicon_Edit/Using_Add_from_Catalog_dialog_box.md) dialog box you would insert a category (such as *Verb*) and *then* insert any subcategories (such as *Transitive* *verb*) to maintain that predefined hierarchy. However, if you inadvertently insert a subcategory such that it appears as a category, you can move that subcategory under the category to restore the hierarchy.
