# Insert a category

*Using Tools › Grammar tools › Category Edit*

1.  In the **Navigation Pane**, click **Grammar**, and then click **Category Edit** or **Categories Browse**.

2.  Do one of the following:

    - On the [Insert](../../../User_Interface/Toolbars/Insert_toolbar.md) toolbar, click ![](../../../assets/images/Using_Tools/Grammar_tools/Category_Edit/Insert_Category.GIF).

    - In the **Insert** menu, click **Category (or Part of Speech)**.

    - Press the [shortcut keys](../../../User_Interface/Shortcuts/shortcut_keys_Grammar_tools.md) **Ctrl+I**.

    The **Add from Catalog** dialog box appears.

3.  Do one of the following:

    - In the **Grammatical Category Catalog** area, select the desired category, and then click **Add**.

    - Click the **Create a custom category** link.

    The selected category or a new (not completed) category appears in **Category (or Part of Speech)** pane.

4.  For a custom category, in the **Category (or Part of Speech)** pane, enter content in the [Name](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/rlt1edc.md), [Abbreviation](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/abbreviation_field_category_category_edit.md) and [Description](../../../User_Interface/Field_Descriptions/Grammar/Category_Edit_fields/description_field_category_category_edit.md) fields to complete it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - Refer to [Category Edit overview](Category_Edit_overview.md) for information about how the hierarchical order of categories and subcategories can affect your language project and for additional topics related to the various fields in the new category.
