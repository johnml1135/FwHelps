# Morphology and Parsing Tasks overview

*Morphology and Parsing Tasks*

[Texts & Words overview](../Using_Tools/Texts_&_Words_tools/Texts_and_Words_overview.md) and [Parsing words overview](../User_Interface/Menus/Parser/Parsing_words_overview.md) list the traditional task help for parsing.

The topics in this Help book are "sample" topics for the [default (XAmple) parser](../User_Interface/Menus/Parser/Parsing_words.md). Each includes one or more examples and then the specific steps appropriate for accomplishing them. You can use these as models to help you determine what you need to do in your particular situations.

These topics are based on examples in *A Conceptual Introduction to Morphological Parsing*. (On the [Help](../User_Interface/Menus/Help/Help_overview.md) menu, point to **Resources**, and then click **Introduction to Parsing**.)
